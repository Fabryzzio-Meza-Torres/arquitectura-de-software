# Objective

Replace a slow, opaque, partly offline financing process with one where:

1. **Requesting financing feels like minutes, not weeks.** A client company submits a
   request in a single short form and sees its status update in real time, with no red tape
   blocking the project from starting.
2. **Every outcome carries a reason.** A rejection, a conditioned approval, or an
   exchange-rate change is never a bare status — the *why* is always attached, and the
   negotiation that led to it (meetings, proposals, documentation) is traceable.
3. **Money is never ambiguous.** At any moment, either party can see currency, exchange rate
   in effect, amount due, amount paid, and outstanding balance for a contract — and every
   rate change is visible with its before/after and reason.
4. **Both terminal decisions happen entirely inside the system.** Neither the credit
   decision, nor the end-of-contract resolution (purchase option vs. equipment return),
   requires a step outside the platform.

This is what "solved" means for the purposes of [Users and their needs](UsersAndTheirNeeds.md)
and the [Acceptance criteria](AcceptanceCriteria.md): a requirement that achieves 1–4 for its
domain fully meets the objective; one that achieves it partially, or only for the happy path,
meets it incompletely.
