# Summary

**Lea$e** is a financing and contract-management platform that mediates exactly one
relationship: a client company that needs machinery for a project, and the leasing company
that finances it. The client company requests financing for equipment already agreed upon
with a provider outside the system; a broker facilitates the negotiation and attaches the
contract documentation; the leasing company evaluates and records a decision; on approval,
the request becomes an active contract with a locked exchange rate and a generated
installment schedule; the client company pays installments over the contract term while the
leasing company collects, reconciles and tracks delinquency; at the end of the term the
client company resolves the contract by either exercising the purchase option or returning
the equipment.

The platform is not a marketplace, a procurement tool, or a fleet-management system — see
[Out of scope](OutOfScope.md). It is narrowly the system of record and workflow engine for
the financing lifecycle between the two parties, per
[Key product decisions](KeyProductDecisions.md) (KPD-1).
