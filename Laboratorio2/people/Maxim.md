# Broker — Negotiation Facilitator

## Who they are

The Broker is a tactical facilitator who sits between the client company's Head of Finance and
the leasing company's Head of Credit and Collections. Unlike the equipment provider, the
Broker **is** an in-system actor — but unlike the two decision-owning roles, the Broker never
owns a decision. This role's job is to close the deal and the contract, with all the pertinent
documentation, as fast as possible.

## Role in the system

Responsible for closing the deal and the contract between the client company and the leasing
company, with the pertinent documentation attached.

## Needs (Jobs to be done)

- Book a negotiation meeting between the client company and the leasing company.
- Propose ideas to close the agreement, based on knowledge of the provider, the client's
  finances and the client's need.
- Send messages to the client company or the leasing company with guidance and advice to close
  the agreement.
- Submit the PDF, summary and details of the contract into the system.

## Pain points

- Main pain point: needs to close the deal and the contract between the client company and
  the leasing company, with all the pertinent documentation, without the process stalling on
  either side.

## Main flows this role participates in (inside the system)

1. **Book a negotiation meeting** between the client company and the leasing company.
2. **Propose deal ideas** to close the agreement.
3. **Submit the PDF, summary and details** of the contract.

See [Main flows](../Core/MainFlows.md), Flow 1B, for the full step-by-step.

## What this role expects from the system

- Visibility into which negotiations are open, proposed, or closed.
- Confirmation that an uploaded PDF is visible to both the client company and the leasing
  company.

## Success criteria

- The Broker can always tell, at a glance, the state of every negotiation being facilitated.
- No negotiation stalls because a document or a meeting proposal got lost.
