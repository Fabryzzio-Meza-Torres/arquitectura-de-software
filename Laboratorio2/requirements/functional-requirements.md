## Functional Requirements — Lea$e (machinery leasing, Peru)

> **Assumed baselines** (to be confirmed in `spec.md`; every figure below traces to one of these):
> **Personas** — *Pedro*: finance manager of an SME lessee (customer). *Carlos*: credit & risk analyst at Lea$e. *Julia*: fleet & asset operations manager at Lea$e. *Supervisor*: risk supervisor (approval authority).
> **Critical problems** — (1) Credit & risk decisioning, (2) Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling, (3) Asset lifecycle & availability.
> **Staged scope** — Phase 1: 1,000 active contracts / 2,000 assets. Phase 2: 100,000 active contracts / 200,000 assets in 6 months. Phase 3: 10,000,000 telemetry events per day in 2 years.

| ID | Functional requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system must allow an SME finance manager (Pedro) to submit a leasing application capturing RUC, machinery type, requested term in months, requested amount in PEN, and the client project's expected settlement date, persisting it in state `SUBMITTED` in under 2 seconds at p95; if any of these five fields is missing or the RUC fails SUNAT 11-digit format validation, the system must reject the submission with a per-field error message and must not create a partial application. If the submission fails due to loss of connectivity, the draft must be stored locally and retried up to 3 times at 10-second intervals before alerting Pedro. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem. |
| FR-02 | The system must allow Pedro to attach the mandatory credit dossier (last 12 months of bank statements, latest annual tax return, and the awarded-project contract or purchase order) to an application in state `SUBMITTED`, accepting PDF/JPG/PNG files of up to 20 MB each, and must block the transition to `IN_REVIEW` while any of the three mandatory documents is absent, showing which one is missing. If a file fails the antivirus scan it must be quarantined, not stored, and Pedro must be notified within 1 minute with the reason. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem. |
| FR-03 | The system must request an external credit-bureau score for every application entering `IN_REVIEW` with a request timeout of 10 seconds, retrying twice with exponential backoff (2 s, 4 s); if the bureau is still unavailable after the third attempt, the application must be moved to state `SCORING_UNAVAILABLE` and routed to Carlos's manual review queue (FR-05) instead of being auto-rejected, and the failure must be recorded in the audit trail of FR-07. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem. |
| FR-04 | The system must produce, for every application, a decision of `APPROVED`, `REJECTED` or `CONDITIONED` within 24 business hours of entering `IN_REVIEW`, storing the approved credit limit in PEN, the maximum term in months, and at least one machine-readable reason code per decision; an application that reaches 20 business hours without a decision must be escalated by notification to the risk supervisor, and one that exceeds 24 business hours must be flagged as `SLA_BREACHED` in Carlos's queue without altering the application state. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem. |
| FR-05 | The system must present to the credit analyst (Carlos) a manual review queue containing every application whose bureau score falls in the configured gray band (default 550–680) or whose state is `SCORING_UNAVAILABLE` (FR-03), ordered by remaining SLA time ascending, and must require a reason code plus a free-text justification of at least 20 characters before allowing any decision to be saved. Applications with a requested amount above 500,000 PEN must additionally require approval by a second user holding the risk supervisor role, and the system must block a user from being both approver and reviewer of the same application. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem. |
| FR-06 | The system must notify Pedro of the decision on his application (FR-04) through email and in-app inbox within 5 minutes of the decision being saved, including the outcome, the approved limit and term when applicable, and the reason codes in customer-readable text; if email delivery fails, the system must retry 3 times over 30 minutes and, regardless of the email outcome, the in-app inbox entry must always be written so that the decision is never lost. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem. |
| FR-07 | The system must record, for every state transition of an application, contract, payment schedule and asset, an append-only audit entry containing actor, role, timestamp with time zone, previous value and new value; audit entries must never be modifiable or deletable by any role, must be queryable by the risk supervisor filtered by contract, actor and date range with a response under 3 seconds, and must remain retrievable after a failure within the recovery window defined in NFR-02. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem. |
| FR-08 | The system must let Pedro generate up to 3 comparable payment-schedule simulations for an `APPROVED` application, each configurable with a grace period of 0 to 6 months, a monthly or milestone-based installment frequency, and an optional balloon payment of up to 40 % of the principal payable at the project settlement date; each simulation must show total interest, total cost and the installment calendar, and the sum of installments must equal principal plus interest with any rounding difference under 0.01 PEN absorbed in the final installment. Simulations expire and are purged 30 days after creation. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-09 | The system must let Pedro align each installment due date of a chosen simulation (FR-08) to a declared project milestone date, rejecting any configuration where the grace period extends past the project settlement date declared in FR-01 or where an installment falls due before the asset handover date of FR-16, and must display the resulting cash-flow gap in days for every installment. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-10 | The system must activate a leasing contract only when all three conditions hold simultaneously: the application is `APPROVED` (FR-04), the payment schedule is accepted and digitally signed by Pedro, and an asset unit is exclusively allocated (FR-14); upon activation the payment schedule is locked and can only change through the restructuring flow of FR-12. If two activation attempts are made for the same application, the second must be rejected with a conflict message and must not create a duplicate contract. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling and Asset lifecycle problems. |
| FR-11 | The system must register incoming payments against a contract's installments using the bank reference as an idempotency key, so that re-processing the same bank reference returns the original receipt without creating a second payment or altering the balance; partial payments must be applied to the oldest outstanding installment first, and any payment that cannot be matched to a contract within 1 business day must be moved to an exceptions queue for operations review instead of being discarded. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-12 | The system must notify Pedro of each upcoming installment through email and SMS 72 hours and again 24 hours before the due date, retrying each channel up to 3 times over 30 minutes on delivery failure; if both channels are exhausted without delivery, the notification must still be written to the in-app inbox and logged as `DEGRADED_DELIVERY` in the audit trail of FR-07. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-13 | The system must apply an automatic dunning ladder to any installment unpaid after its due date, sending reminders at day +1, +8, +15 and +30, transitioning the contract from `ACTIVE` to `OVERDUE` at day +8 and from `OVERDUE` to `DEFAULT` at day +90, and halting the ladder immediately when a payment registered by FR-11 settles the outstanding installment; every transition must notify Pedro and the assigned analyst within 5 minutes and must be recorded in FR-07. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-14 | The system must let Pedro request one schedule restructuring per rolling 12-month period for a contract in `ACTIVE` or `OVERDUE` state, stating the reason and the proposed new milestone dates; Carlos must decide within 5 business days, and an approved restructuring generates a new schedule that supersedes the previous one while the previous schedule and all its payments are retained as historical records and remain visible. A contract in `DEFAULT` is not eligible and the request must be rejected with that reason. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-15 | The system must allocate a specific machinery unit to a contract with an exclusive lock over the requested date range, guaranteeing that no unit is allocated to two contracts with overlapping ranges; a concurrent allocation request for the same unit and overlapping range must fail within 2 seconds with a conflict message and must propose the next available unit of the same type and capacity, or the earliest available date for that unit if no alternative exists. Serves the Asset lifecycle & availability problem. |
| FR-16 | The system must record the handover of an allocated unit to Pedro's site through a checklist capturing hour-meter reading, at least 4 condition photographs, accessories delivered, and the digital signatures of Julia's operator and of Pedro's site representative; the contract's billing start date must not be set and the schedule of FR-10 must not begin until the checklist is complete and both signatures are captured. The same checklist structure applies to the return of the unit. Serves the Asset lifecycle & availability problem. |
| FR-17 | The system must publish a machinery catalog filterable by type, capacity, branch and date range, showing for every unit its availability calendar at day granularity and its current state (`AVAILABLE`, `ALLOCATED`, `IN_MAINTENANCE`, `IN_RECOVERY`); when no unit matches the requested range the catalog must explicitly show the earliest available date per matching unit instead of an empty result. Serves the Asset lifecycle & availability problem. |
| FR-18 | The system must ingest telemetry from each deployed unit every 15 minutes containing GPS position, engine hours and fault codes, and must persist it as the unit's current state; if the unit loses connectivity, the device must buffer readings locally for up to 72 hours and backfill them in chronological order on reconnection, and any unit with no reading for more than 72 hours must raise an exception ticket to Julia within 15 minutes of crossing that threshold. Serves the Asset lifecycle & availability problem. |
| FR-19 | The system must schedule preventive maintenance for a unit when it reaches every 250 engine hours reported by FR-18 or 6 calendar months since the last service, whichever occurs first, moving the unit to `IN_MAINTENANCE` and excluding it from allocation (FR-15) for the scheduled window; if the maintenance window overlaps an existing allocation, the system must block the automatic scheduling and notify Julia to arrange a replacement unit rather than interrupting an active contract. Serves the Asset lifecycle & availability problem. |
| FR-20 | The system must open a recovery order for every contract entering `DEFAULT` (FR-13), setting the unit to `IN_RECOVERY`, attaching its last known position and engine hours from FR-18, and assigning it to Julia's operations queue; the contract may only be closed once the return checklist of FR-16 is completed, and the unit returns to `AVAILABLE` only after a maintenance inspection under FR-19. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling and Asset lifecycle problems. |
| FR-21 | The system must restrict actions and visible data by role — Applicant (Pedro), Credit Analyst (Carlos), Fleet Operations (Julia) and Risk Supervisor — according to the permission matrix defined in NFR-06, protecting the integrity of credit-decision records, payment schedules and asset allocation data; every unauthorized access attempt must be recorded in the audit trail of FR-07 and must return "Unauthorized access — contact your administrator" without exposing any of the requested data. Serves all three critical problems as a cross-cutting requirement. |
