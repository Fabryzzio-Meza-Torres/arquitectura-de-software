## Functional Requirements — Lea$e (machinery leasing, Peru)

| ID | Functional requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system must allow a client company's Head of Finance to submit a leasing application capturing RUC, machinery type, requested term in months, requested amount, the contract currency (PEN or USD, per KPD-4 — Flow 1 step 3), and the expected settlement date of the client company's project, persisting it in state `SUBMITTED` in under 2 seconds at p95 at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts and a handful of concurrent users (Staged Scope, Phase 1). If any of these six fields is missing, the RUC fails SUNAT 11-digit format validation, or the currency is neither PEN nor USD, the system must reject the submission with a per-field error message and must not create a partial application (AC-1.2). An identical resubmission of the same request (same RUC, machinery type, amount, term and currency, submitted again before a decision is recorded) must not create a duplicate application — the system must return the existing application instead (idempotent submit, AC-1.3). If the submission fails due to loss of connectivity, the draft must be stored locally and retried up to 3 times at 10-second intervals before alerting the client company. Serves Flow 1 and the Financing request → documented decision problem. |
| FR-02 | The system must allow the client company to attach the mandatory credit dossier (last 12 months of bank statements, latest annual tax return, and the awarded-project contract or purchase order) to an application in state `SUBMITTED`, accepting PDF/JPG/PNG files of up to 20 MB each at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1) with each upload processed under 5 seconds at p95, and must block the transition to `IN_REVIEW` while any of the three mandatory documents is absent, showing which one is missing. If a file fails the antivirus scan it must be quarantined, not stored, and the client company must be notified within 1 minute with the reason. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem (Flow 1). |
| FR-03 | The system must request an external credit-bureau score for every application entering `IN_REVIEW` with a request timeout of 10 seconds, retrying twice with exponential backoff (2 s, 4 s), sustaining the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of applications (Staged Scope, Phase 1) and scaling toward NFR-03's Phase 3 throughput target as adoption grows; if the bureau is still unavailable after the third attempt, the application must be moved to state `SCORING_UNAVAILABLE` and routed to the leasing company's manual review queue (FR-05) instead of being auto-rejected, and the failure must be recorded in the audit trail of FR-07. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem (Flow 2). |
| FR-04 | The system must produce, for every application, a decision of `APPROVED`, `REJECTED` or `CONDITIONED` within 24 business hours of entering `IN_REVIEW` (AC-2.4), storing the approved credit limit, the maximum term in months, and at least one machine-readable reason code per decision (AC-2.2), at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of applications (Staged Scope, Phase 1); an application that reaches 20 business hours without a decision must trigger a notification to the leasing company, and one that exceeds 24 business hours must be flagged as `SLA_BREACHED` in the leasing company's manual review queue (FR-05) without altering the application state — the request remains `IN_REVIEW`, never silently disappearing (AC-2.4). Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem (Flow 2). |
| FR-05 | The system must provide the leasing company with a manual review queue containing every application whose bureau score falls in the configured gray band (default 550–680) or whose state is `SCORING_UNAVAILABLE` (FR-03), ordered by remaining SLA time ascending, and must require a reason code plus a free-text justification of at least 20 characters before allowing any decision to be saved; an application requiring dual approval under the threshold and account-separation rule of NFR-07 must not be saved as decided until both required approvals are recorded. Queue ordering and decision-recording must complete under 2 seconds at p95 at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1). Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem (Flow 2). |
| FR-06 | The system must notify the client company of the decision on its application (FR-04) through email and in-app inbox within 5 minutes of the decision being saved, including the outcome, the approved limit and term when applicable, and the reason codes in client-readable text, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of decisions per day (Staged Scope, Phase 1); if email delivery fails, the system must retry 3 times over 30 minutes and, regardless of the email outcome, the in-app inbox entry must always be written so that the decision is never lost. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem (Flow 2). |
| FR-07 | The system must record, for every state transition of an application, negotiation, contract and payment schedule, an append-only audit entry containing actor, organization, timestamp with time zone, previous value and new value; audit entries must never be modifiable or deletable by either company or the Broker, must be queryable by the leasing company filtered by contract, actor and date range with a response under 3 seconds at p95 at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1), scaling to Phase 3's thousands-of-contracts audit volume (Staged Scope, Phase 3) without a change in query latency, and must remain retrievable after a failure within the recovery window defined in NFR-02. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem as the cross-cutting traceability requirement of KPD-6. |
| FR-08 | The system must let the client company generate up to 3 comparable payment-schedule simulations for an `APPROVED` application, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1) — this precedes contract activation (Flow 3) and is therefore a Phase 1 capability — each configurable with a grace period of 0 to 6 months, a monthly or milestone-based installment frequency, and an optional balloon payment of up to 40 % of the principal payable at the project settlement date; each simulation must show total interest, total cost, the installment calendar, and the total capital outlay avoided compared to an outright cash purchase of the requested amount (the requested amount minus the total scheduled installments due beyond the current period), making explicit how leasing preserves the client company's working capital. The sum of installments must equal principal plus interest with any rounding difference under 0.01 in the contract currency absorbed in the final installment. A 4th simulation request for the same application must be rejected with a message naming the 3-simulation limit. Simulations expire and are purged 30 days after creation. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem and the Head of Finance's need to obtain machinery without tying up capital. |
| FR-09 | The system must let the client company align each installment due date of a chosen simulation (FR-08) to a declared project milestone date, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1), rejecting any configuration where the grace period extends past the project settlement date declared in FR-01, and must display the resulting cash-flow gap in days for every installment. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-10 | The system must activate a leasing contract only when the application is `APPROVED` (FR-04) and a payment schedule (FR-08/FR-09) is accepted and digitally signed by the client company. Upon activation the system must: lock the exchange rate in effect at that moment as the contract's first rate-history entry with an effective date equal to the activation timestamp (KPD-4, AC-3.2); price and lock the chosen schedule at that rate (AC-3.3), which can only change afterward through the restructuring flow of FR-14; and make the new contract immediately visible in the Head of Credit and Collections' portfolio (FR-27). Retried or concurrent activation of the same application must produce exactly one contract and one schedule — a second attempt must be rejected with a conflict message rather than creating a duplicate (AC-3.4), verified at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts by the concurrency test in NFR-04. Serves Flow 3 (the POC's committed Happy Path, KPD-7) and Critical problem 2(a). |
| FR-11 | The system must register incoming payments against a contract's installments using the bank reference as an idempotency key, so that re-processing the same bank reference returns the original receipt without creating a second payment or altering the balance (AC-4.3); partial payments must be applied to the oldest outstanding installment first, and any payment whose amount differs from the scheduled installment amount for its period — whether partial or over payment — must be flagged as a `RECONCILIATION_MISMATCH` visible to the leasing company rather than silently accepted (AC-4.2), while still being applied to the balance per the oldest-first rule. Any payment that cannot be matched to a contract within 1 business day must be moved to the leasing company's exceptions queue instead of being discarded. This must hold at the Phase 2 volume of hundreds of active contracts across PEN and USD (Staged Scope, Phase 2), with registration and reconciliation completing under 3 seconds at p95, serializable per the concurrency test of NFR-04. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem and Critical problem 2(b). |
| FR-12 | The system must notify the client company of each upcoming installment through email and SMS 72 hours and again 24 hours before the due date, at the Phase 2 volume of hundreds of active contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 2), retrying each channel up to 3 times over 30 minutes on delivery failure; if both channels are exhausted without delivery, the notification must still be written to the in-app inbox and logged as `DEGRADED_DELIVERY` in the audit trail of FR-07. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-13 | The system must apply an automatic dunning ladder to any installment unpaid after its due date, sending reminders at day +1, +15, +30, +45 and +60, transitioning the contract from `ACTIVE` to `OVERDUE` at day +1 (the first missed due date) and from `OVERDUE` to `DEFAULT` at day +61, and halting the ladder immediately when a payment registered by FR-11 settles the outstanding installment; every transition must notify the client company and the leasing company within 5 minutes and must be recorded in FR-07. The `OVERDUE` state (day +1 through day +60) always exactly spans the Yellow and Orange delinquency colors of FR-19, and the `DEFAULT` state (day +61 onward) always exactly corresponds to Red, so a contract's state and its delinquency color are never in conflict for the same elapsed time. This is the Phase 2 full delinquency/dunning automation capability (Staged Scope, Phase 2, hundreds of active contracts); each reminder must dispatch within 5 minutes of its day-boundary being crossed at that volume. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem and Critical problem 2(b). |
| FR-14 | The system must let the client company request one schedule restructuring per rolling 12-month period for a contract in `ACTIVE` or `OVERDUE` state, at the Phase 2 volume of hundreds of active contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 2), stating the reason and the proposed new milestone dates; the leasing company must decide within 5 business days, and an approved restructuring generates a new schedule that supersedes the previous one while the previous schedule and all its payments are retained as historical records and remain visible. Two simultaneous restructuring requests for the same contract must be serialized so exactly one is accepted for evaluation at a time, the second being rejected with a conflict message until the first is decided. A contract in `DEFAULT` is not eligible and the request must be rejected with that reason. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem. |
| FR-15 | The system must restrict actions and visible data among the Client Company, Leasing Company and Broker according to the permission rules defined in NFR-06 (RBAC matrix), NFR-15 (Broker scope) and NFR-16 (leasing-company-exclusive features), protecting the integrity of credit-decision records, negotiation records, contract documents and payment schedules; every unauthorized access attempt must be recorded in the audit trail of FR-07 and must return "Unauthorized access" without exposing any of the requested data. This holds from Phase 1 onward at every Staged Scope volume, since access control cannot be deferred to a later phase. Serves all three critical problems as a cross-cutting requirement. |
| FR-16 | The system must let a Broker book a negotiation meeting between the client company and the leasing company for an application in `SUBMITTED` or `IN_REVIEW` state, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1), and must let either company accept, reject or propose a new date; a rejected date must not close the negotiation, and the Broker may propose another, up to a maximum of 5 proposed dates per negotiation — the 6th consecutive rejection must flag the negotiation as `STALLED` to both companies rather than allowing an unbounded loop. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem (Flow 1B). |
| FR-17 | The system must let a Broker upload the contract's PDF (up to 20 MB), a text summary and its structured details against an application, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1) with each upload processed under 5 seconds at p95; once uploaded, both the client company and the leasing company must be able to view the same document and details. An upload missing the summary or the structured details must be rejected as incomplete. Serves the Credit & risk decisioning problem (Flow 1B). |
| FR-18 | The system must let the client company confirm or reject the reception of the machinery through a contract status update, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1); a confirmed reception must be recorded against the contract, and a rejected reception must be flagged to the leasing company instead of silently passing as confirmed. The Provider must not be exposed as an actor, screen, API or flow. Serves Flow 3 and the scope constraint of KPD-2. |
| FR-19 | The system must classify every active contract's delinquency into exactly 4 levels based on elapsed time since the missed due date — Green (paid on time), Yellow (day +1 to +30, 1 month unpaid), Orange (day +31 to +60, 2 months unpaid), Red (day +61 onward, more than 2 months unpaid) — recomputing the level daily under the ACID guarantee of NFR-17, and must let the leasing company send a formal message to a client company whose contract is Yellow, Orange or Red. This classification engine is introduced in Phase 1 as a dormant capability (Staged Scope, Phase 1 — no contract carries a non-Green level before any payment is due) and becomes operationally active once payments begin in Phase 2, at the volume of hundreds of active contracts, with the daily recompute completing under 10 seconds at p95. In Phase 1, sending the formal message is a single leasing-company-triggered action (VG3); the automated day-based reminder ladder of FR-13 is the Phase 2 capability layered on top of this classification. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem, Critical problem 2(b), and VG3. |
| FR-20 | The system must compute the pronosticated income of the current month for the leasing company as the sum of every active contract's installment due within the month, assuming full payment, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1) scaling to Phase 2's hundreds of active contracts, and must expose it as a single figure refreshed at least once per day; a contract in `OVERDUE` or `DEFAULT` still contributes its scheduled installment to the sum (the figure assumes full payment by definition), and if the daily refresh job fails the system must show the last successfully computed figure with its computation timestamp rather than a blank or zero value. Serves the Cash-flow-aligned payment scheduling problem and VG3. |
| FR-21 | The system must resolve the end of a contract term through exactly one of two mutually exclusive branches chosen by the client company, at the Phase 3 volume of thousands of contracts over the portfolio's life (Staged Scope, Phase 3): the purchase option (allowed only once all remaining installments, including any outstanding delinquency, are paid) or the equipment return (no purchase); the system must reject an attempt to select both, and the resolved contract must move to a closed state (`COMPLETED_PURCHASED` or `COMPLETED_RETURNED`) and appear in the leasing company's closed-agreements history within 2 seconds at p95. Serves the end-of-contract resolution problem (Flow 6, VG4). |
| FR-22 | The system must let the Broker submit a non-binding deal proposal for a negotiation in `OPEN` or `PROPOSED` state, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1), recording the proposal text, timestamp and related application and making it visible to both the client company and the leasing company; the proposal must not modify the application decision, contract terms or negotiation state, and an attempt to submit one for a `CLOSED` negotiation must be rejected while preserving prior proposals. Serves the Broker's deal-facilitation need in Flow 1B without granting decision authority. |
| FR-23 | The system must let the Broker send guidance messages to the client company, the leasing company or both within the negotiation being facilitated, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1), preserving each message with its recipients, timestamp and delivery status; a message must not approve an application, accept a proposal or alter contract terms, and if delivery fails it must remain visible in the negotiation thread with status `DELIVERY_FAILED` for retry. Serves the Broker's communication need in Flow 1B without granting decision authority. |
| FR-24 | The system must provide the Broker with a view of only the negotiations being facilitated, at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1), showing for each one its `OPEN`, `PROPOSED` or `CLOSED` state, next meeting date, contract-document status and last recorded activity; the view must reflect the latest persisted state with no more than 5 seconds of staleness, and when no negotiations are assigned it must show an explicit empty state without exposing other negotiations or the leasing company's portfolio. Serves the Broker's negotiation-visibility need in Flow 1B. |
| FR-25 | The system must let the Head of Credit and Collections apply an exchange-rate change to a contract in `ACTIVE` state, at the Phase 2 volume of hundreds of active contracts across PEN and USD (Staged Scope, Phase 2): recording the new rate as a rate-history entry with an effective date and a free-text reason of at least 10 characters, while preserving the prior rate value unchanged in history (KPD-4, AC-5.1); recomputing only installments due on or after the effective date, never rewriting or re-reconciling installments already reconciled before that date (AC-5.2); notifying the Head of Finance within 5 minutes of the change with the before rate, after rate, effective date and reason (AC-5.3); and making the full rate-change history retrievable by both the Head of Finance and the Head of Credit and Collections at any time (AC-5.4). Recomputation must complete under 3 seconds at p95 at that volume; two concurrent rate-change requests for the same contract must be serialized so exactly one is applied and the second is rejected with a conflict message, preventing a lost update. Serves Flow 5 and Critical problem 2(a). |
| FR-26 | The system must let the Head of Finance view, at any time after contract activation, their own active contract's installment schedule, outstanding balance, contract currency and exchange rate currently in effect (AC-3.5, AC-7.2), and must let the Head of Credit and Collections view the same figures for any contract in the collections portfolio, per contract; the view must reflect the latest persisted state with no more than 5 seconds of staleness — a rate change (FR-25) or a payment (FR-11) applied while the view is open must be reflected on the next refresh without requiring re-login. This must return in under 2 seconds at p95 at the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 1), scaling to Phase 2's hundreds of active contracts without a change in behavior. Serves Flow 3 step 4, the Head of Finance's and the Head of Credit and Collections' need to see the installment schedule per contract (Users and their needs), and Critical problem 2. |
| FR-27 | The system must provide the Head of Credit and Collections with a portfolio-level dashboard aggregating, across every contract: total amounts receivable by currency, the count and value of contracts in each of the 4 delinquency colors (FR-19), and the leasing company's total currency exposure (the sum of outstanding balances by currency and rate in effect); the dashboard must refresh at least once per day and must exclude any closed contract (`COMPLETED_PURCHASED` / `COMPLETED_RETURNED`) from the at-risk and exposure totals while retaining it in historical reporting. This is feasible at the Phase 3 volume of thousands of contracts over the portfolio's life (Staged Scope, Phase 3), returning in under 5 seconds at p95 for a portfolio of up to 5,000 contracts; if the aggregation job fails, the dashboard must show the last successfully computed figures with their computation timestamp rather than a blank or zero value. Access to this dashboard is restricted to the leasing company per NFR-16. Serves the Head of Credit and Collections' portfolio-level-visibility need (Users and their needs) and Critical problem 2. |
