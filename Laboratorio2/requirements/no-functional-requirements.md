## Non-Functional Requirements — Lea$e (machinery leasing, Peru)

| ID | Non-functional requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The system must sustain a minimum monthly availability of 99.9 % for the application, negotiation, contract and payment APIs, measured at the load balancer over the total minutes of the month and excluding scheduled maintenance windows (announced at least 48 hours in advance and capped at 4 hours per month); during a partial outage the system must degrade to read-only access to applications, negotiations, contracts and schedules rather than returning errors, and must queue write operations for replay once the failed component recovers. This protects the availability of the credit-decision, Broker-negotiation and payment-schedule data of FR-04, FR-16, FR-17 and FR-22 through FR-24. |
| NFR-02 | The system must restore its critical functions (application submission, negotiation consultation, decision consultation, contract activation and payment registration) in under 5 minutes (RTO) after a total or partial component failure, with a maximum data loss of 1 minute (RPO), retrying automatic reconnection every 15 seconds during that window; if the primary region is unavailable for more than 5 minutes, traffic must fail over to the secondary region and the failover must be announced to the leasing company within 1 minute. |
| NFR-03 | The credit decisioning pipeline of FR-03 and FR-04 must process at least 2,000 applications per hour with an end-to-end automated decision latency under 30 seconds at p95, measured from entering `IN_REVIEW` to the decision being persisted; when the external bureau's response time exceeds 10 seconds or its error rate exceeds 5 % over a 5-minute window, the system must open a circuit breaker and route new applications to the manual queue of FR-05 instead of accumulating pending requests. |
| NFR-04 | Contract activation (FR-10) and payment registration (FR-11) must be serializable, guaranteeing zero duplicate contracts or schedules for the same approved application and zero duplicate charges for the same bank reference, verified by a concurrency test of 100 simultaneous requests for the same application and bank reference in which exactly one activation and one payment succeed; when the consistency guarantee cannot be upheld the operation must be rejected with a conflict response rather than committed optimistically. |
| NFR-05 | The system must require individual credential authentication with a second factor for every leasing-company account and enforce this minimum permission matrix: Client Company (read/write only on its own applications, negotiations, contracts, schedules and simulations), Leasing Company (read/write on applications, decisions, negotiations, contracts and payment schedules, plus read access to the audit trail), and Broker (read/write only on meetings, proposals, messages and documents for negotiations being facilitated). Access is denied by default for any permission not listed, and access to one client company's data must never expose another client company's data. Applications above 500,000 PEN must require approval through two distinct authorized leasing-company accounts. After 5 failed authentication attempts within 10 minutes the account must be locked for 15 minutes and the leasing company notified. |
| NFR-06 | Credit-dossier documents, payment data and personal data must be transmitted encrypted over TLS 1.3 (TLS 1.2 minimum for legacy integrations) and stored encrypted at rest with AES-256; encryption keys must be rotated every 90 days and, on suspicion of compromise, must be revocable and regenerable without data loss and without an availability interruption beyond the budget defined in NFR-01. |
| NFR-07 | Audit records (FR-07), credit decisions and contract documents must be retained in immutable storage for at least 10 years to satisfy Peruvian financial recordkeeping obligations, and must remain retrievable within 5 minutes for records under 2 years old and within 24 hours for archived records; any attempt to modify or delete a retained record must be rejected and reported as a security event under NFR-08. |
| NFR-08 | The system must emit structured logs, metrics and traces for every credit decision, Broker negotiation action, contract activation and payment registration, and must raise an alert to the leasing company within 2 minutes when any of these thresholds is crossed: API error rate above 1 % over 5 minutes, decision SLA breaches (FR-04) above 5 per hour, or failed negotiation-message deliveries (FR-23) above 20 per hour; if the observability backend is unavailable, the system must buffer observability data locally for 1 hour and must not block or degrade the business transaction it is observing. |
| NFR-09 | Personal and financial data submitted by client companies must be processed in compliance with Peruvian Personal Data Protection Law N.º 29733, restricting credit-dossier access to authorized leasing-company accounts under NFR-05, honoring a data-access or rectification request within 10 business days, and masking document identifiers and bank references in all logs and non-production environments; if an export of personal data is requested by an unauthorized account it must be blocked and recorded as a security event. |
| NFR-10 | The client-company-facing flows (FR-01, FR-08, FR-12) must be usable on a mobile browser at 360 px width and must meet WCAG 2.1 level AA for contrast, keyboard navigation and form labeling, verified by an automated accessibility scan with zero level-A or level-AA violations on those screens; when the connection drops mid-form, entered data must be preserved locally and restored on the next session rather than lost. |
| NFR-11 | The system must restrict a Broker to read/write only on the meetings, documents, proposals and messages of negotiations (FR-16, FR-17, FR-22 and FR-23) being actively facilitated and to view only those negotiations through FR-24; it must restrict the pronosticated-income figure (FR-20), delinquency engine (FR-19), credit decisions and payment schedules to the client company and leasing company as applicable. Any unauthorized read or write attempt must be denied by default and recorded in the audit trail of FR-07. |
| NFR-12 | The delinquency-level recomputation (FR-19) must run under an ACID transaction per contract, guaranteeing that a payment registered in the same window that settles the outstanding installment halts the collections-message trigger immediately, verified by a concurrency test of 50 simultaneous payment-vs-recompute operations on the same contract in which the final delinquency level always reflects the settled payment. |
| NFR-13 | Once a contract transitions to `ACTIVE`, the system must reject any write attempt to its currency or to an existing exchange-rate history entry at the database level, while allowing a new rate entry with an effective date through Flow 5; a rejected overwrite must return a conflict response rather than silently ignoring the write. This protects the currency immutability and rate-history guarantees of KPD-4. |
