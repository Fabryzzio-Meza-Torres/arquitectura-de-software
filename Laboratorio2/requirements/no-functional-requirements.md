## Non-Functional Requirements — Lea$e (machinery leasing, Peru)

| ID | Non-functional requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The system must sustain a minimum monthly availability of 99.9 % for the application, negotiation, contract and payment APIs, measured at the load balancer over the total minutes of the month and excluding scheduled maintenance windows (announced at least 48 hours in advance and capped at 4 hours per month), from the Phase 1 POC volume of tens of contracts and a handful of concurrent users through Phase 3's thousands of contracts (Staged Scope) without a change to the target; during a partial outage the system must degrade to read-only access to applications, negotiations, contracts and schedules rather than returning errors, and must queue write operations for replay once the failed component recovers. This protects the availability of the credit-decision, Broker-negotiation and payment-schedule data of FR-04, FR-16, FR-17 and FR-22 through FR-24. |
| NFR-02 | The system must restore its critical functions (application submission, negotiation consultation, decision consultation, contract activation and payment registration) in under 5 minutes (RTO) after a total or partial component failure, with a maximum data loss of 1 minute (RPO), at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1's tens of contracts through Phase 3's thousands of contracts, retrying automatic reconnection every 15 seconds during that window; if the primary region is unavailable for more than 5 minutes, traffic must fail over to the secondary region and the failover must be announced to the leasing company within 1 minute. |
| NFR-03 | The credit decisioning pipeline of FR-03 and FR-04 must sustain an end-to-end automated decision latency under 30 seconds at p95, measured from entering `IN_REVIEW` to the decision being persisted, at a minimum throughput of one application per minute for the Phase 1 POC (tens of contracts, a handful of concurrent users — Staged Scope, Phase 1), scaling to at least 2,000 applications per hour at the same 30-second p95 latency by Phase 3's portfolio scale (thousands of contracts over the portfolio's life — Staged Scope, Phase 3); when the external bureau's response time exceeds 10 seconds or its error rate exceeds 5 % over a 5-minute window, the system must open a circuit breaker and route new applications to the manual queue of FR-05 instead of accumulating pending requests. |
| NFR-04 | Contract activation (FR-10) and payment registration (FR-11) must be serializable, guaranteeing zero duplicate contracts or schedules for the same approved application and zero duplicate charges for the same bank reference, at the Phase 1 volume of tens of contracts scaling to Phase 2's hundreds of active contracts (Staged Scope), verified by a concurrency test of 100 simultaneous requests for the same application and bank reference in which exactly one activation and one payment succeed; when the consistency guarantee cannot be upheld the operation must be rejected with a conflict response rather than committed optimistically. This verifies, and adds no business rule beyond, the idempotency guarantees already stated in FR-10 and FR-11. |
| NFR-05 | The system must require individual credential authentication with a second factor for every leasing-company account, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward; after 5 failed authentication attempts within 10 minutes the account must be locked for 15 minutes and the leasing company notified within 1 minute of the lockout. |
| NFR-06 | The system must enforce this minimum permission matrix, denying access by default for any permission not listed and never exposing one client company's data to another: Client Company (read/write only on its own applications, negotiations, contracts, schedules and simulations), Leasing Company (read/write on applications, decisions, negotiations, contracts and payment schedules, plus read access to the audit trail), and Broker (read/write only on meetings, proposals, messages and documents for negotiations being facilitated, per NFR-15). This matrix must hold at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward and is the basis for FR-15's cross-cutting access control. |
| NFR-07 | Applications with a requested financing amount above 500,000 PEN (or its USD equivalent at the rate in effect) must require approval through two distinct authorized leasing-company accounts before the decision of FR-05 can be saved, and the system must block the same account from both reviewing and approving the same application. This threshold holds at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward; the account-separation check must complete under 1 second at p95. |
| NFR-08 | Credit-dossier documents, payment data and personal data must be transmitted encrypted over TLS 1.3 (TLS 1.2 minimum for legacy integrations) and stored encrypted at rest with AES-256, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1's tens of contracts through Phase 3's thousands of contracts; encryption keys must be rotated every 90 days and, on suspicion of compromise, must be revocable and regenerable without data loss and without an availability interruption beyond the budget defined in NFR-01. |
| NFR-09 | Audit records (FR-07), credit decisions and contract documents must be retained in immutable storage for at least 10 years to satisfy Peruvian financial recordkeeping obligations, at every Staged Scope phase, and must remain retrievable within 5 minutes for records under 2 years old and within 24 hours for archived records; any attempt to modify or delete a retained record must be rejected and reported as a security event under NFR-10. |
| NFR-10 | The system must emit structured logs, metrics and traces for every credit decision, Broker negotiation action, contract activation and payment registration, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1's tens of contracts through Phase 3's thousands of contracts, and must raise an alert to the leasing company within 2 minutes when any of these thresholds is crossed: API error rate above 1 % over 5 minutes, decision SLA breaches (FR-04) above 5 per hour, or failed negotiation-message deliveries (FR-23) above 20 per hour; if the observability backend is unavailable, the system must buffer observability data locally for 1 hour and must not block or degrade the business transaction it is observing. |
| NFR-11 | Personal and financial data submitted by client companies must be processed in compliance with Peruvian Personal Data Protection Law N.º 29733, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward: restricting credit-dossier access to authorized leasing-company accounts under NFR-06, and honoring a data-access or rectification request within 10 business days. |
| NFR-12 | Document identifiers and bank references must be masked in all logs and non-production environments, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward; if an export of personal data is requested by an account not authorized under NFR-06, the export must be blocked and the attempt recorded as a security event under NFR-10. |
| NFR-13 | The client-company-facing flows (FR-01, FR-08, FR-12) must be usable on a mobile browser at 360 px width and must meet WCAG 2.1 level AA for contrast, keyboard navigation and form labeling, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward, verified by an automated accessibility scan with zero level-A or level-AA violations on those screens. |
| NFR-14 | When the connection drops mid-form on the client-company-facing flows (FR-01, FR-08, FR-12), entered data must be preserved locally and restored on the next session rather than lost, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward, with local preservation surviving at least 24 hours before expiring. |
| NFR-15 | The system must restrict a Broker to read/write only on the meetings, documents, proposals and messages of negotiations (FR-16, FR-17, FR-22 and FR-23) being actively facilitated and to view only those negotiations through FR-24, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward; any unauthorized read or write attempt must be denied by default and recorded in the audit trail of FR-07. |
| NFR-16 | The system must restrict the pronosticated-income figure (FR-20), the delinquency classification engine (FR-19), credit decisions and payment schedules to the client company (its own data only) and the leasing company, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward; the Broker must never read these regardless of which negotiation it facilitates. Any unauthorized read or write attempt must be denied by default and recorded in the audit trail of FR-07. |
| NFR-17 | The delinquency-level recomputation (FR-19) must run under an ACID transaction per contract, at the Phase 2 volume of hundreds of active contracts (Staged Scope, Phase 2), guaranteeing that a payment registered in the same window that settles the outstanding installment halts the collections-message trigger immediately, verified by a concurrency test of 50 simultaneous payment-vs-recompute operations on the same contract in which the final delinquency level always reflects the settled payment. |
| NFR-18 | Once a contract transitions to `ACTIVE` (its exchange rate locked and recorded by FR-10), the system must reject any write attempt to its currency or to an existing exchange-rate history entry at the database level, while allowing a new rate entry with an effective date through FR-25, at every Staged Scope phase from Phase 1 onward; a rejected overwrite must return a conflict response rather than silently ignoring the write. This protects the currency immutability and rate-history guarantees of KPD-4. |
