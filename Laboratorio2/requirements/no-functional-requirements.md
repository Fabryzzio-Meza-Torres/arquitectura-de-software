## Non-Functional Requirements — Lea$e (machinery leasing, Peru)

> Each requirement is written as a quality-attribute scenario: **attribute + metric + threshold + measurement condition + degraded-mode behavior**, as required by the NFR reading of Block D in `agents/eval-spec.md`. Staged-scope figures are the ones declared in `requirements/functional-requirements.md`.

| ID | Non-functional requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The system must sustain a minimum monthly availability of 99.9 % for the contract, payment and allocation APIs, measured at the load balancer over the total minutes of the month and excluding scheduled maintenance windows (announced at least 48 hours in advance and capped at 4 hours per month); during a partial outage the system must degrade to read-only access to contracts, schedules and asset states rather than returning errors, and must queue write operations for replay once the failed component recovers. This protects the availability of the credit-decision, payment-schedule and asset-allocation data of FR-04, FR-10 and FR-15. |
| NFR-02 | The system must restore its critical functions (application submission, decision consultation, payment registration and asset allocation) in under 5 minutes (RTO) after a total or partial component failure, with a maximum data loss of 1 minute (RPO), retrying automatic reconnection every 15 seconds during that window; if the primary region is unavailable for more than 5 minutes, traffic must fail over to the secondary region and the failover must be announced to the operations team within 1 minute. |
| NFR-03 | The machinery catalog and availability query of FR-17 must respond in under 1 second at p95 with up to 500 concurrent users per branch; above that concurrency the system must degrade by serving availability from a cache no older than 60 seconds and displaying the cache timestamp on screen, never exceeding 3 seconds of response time and never failing silently. |
| NFR-04 | The credit decisioning pipeline of FR-03 and FR-04 must process at least 2,000 applications per hour with an end-to-end automated decision latency under 30 seconds at p95, measured from entering `IN_REVIEW` to the decision being persisted; when the external bureau's response time exceeds 10 seconds or its error rate exceeds 5 % over a 5-minute window, the system must open a circuit breaker and route new applications to the manual queue of FR-05 instead of accumulating pending requests. |
| NFR-05 | The allocation of an asset unit (FR-15) and the registration of a payment (FR-11) must be serializable, guaranteeing zero double-bookings of the same unit over overlapping date ranges and zero duplicate charges for the same bank reference, verified by a concurrency test of 100 simultaneous requests over the same unit and the same bank reference in which exactly one allocation and one payment succeed; when the consistency guarantee cannot be upheld the operation must be rejected with a conflict response rather than committed optimistically. |
| NFR-06 | The system must require individual credential authentication with a second factor for the Credit Analyst and Risk Supervisor roles, and enforce this minimum permission matrix: Applicant (read/write on their own applications, contracts, schedules and simulations only), Credit Analyst (read on assigned applications and their dossiers, write of decisions within their limit), Fleet Operations (read/write on assets, allocations, handovers, maintenance and recovery orders; no access to credit dossiers), Risk Supervisor (global read on their portfolio, approval above 500,000 PEN, read on audit trail). Access is denied by default for any permission not listed. After 5 failed authentication attempts within 10 minutes the account must be locked for 15 minutes and the administrator notified. |
| NFR-07 | Credit-dossier documents, payment data and personal data must be transmitted encrypted over TLS 1.3 (TLS 1.2 minimum for legacy integrations) and stored encrypted at rest with AES-256; encryption keys must be rotated every 90 days and, on suspicion of compromise, must be revocable and regenerable without data loss and without an availability interruption beyond the budget defined in NFR-01. |
| NFR-08 | The system must support growth from 1,000 active contracts and 2,000 assets at launch to 100,000 contracts and 200,000 assets within 6 months, and to 10,000,000 telemetry events per day (FR-18) within 2 years, absorbing each phase by horizontal scaling and configuration without code changes and without downtime beyond NFR-01; if onboarding a new branch or asset fleet fails, the operation must roll back without affecting branches already in operation and must notify the platform team. |
| NFR-09 | The telemetry ingestion pipeline of FR-18 must accept a sustained load of 10,000,000 events per day with a peak of 500 events per second and persist each event within 60 seconds of reception at p95; when the ingestion rate exceeds the provisioned capacity the pipeline must buffer events in a durable queue for up to 24 hours and process them in chronological order on recovery, never dropping events and never blocking the device-side buffer of FR-18. |
| NFR-10 | Audit records (FR-07), credit decisions and contract documents must be retained in immutable storage for at least 10 years to satisfy Peruvian financial recordkeeping obligations, and must remain retrievable within 5 minutes for records under 2 years old and within 24 hours for archived records; any attempt to modify or delete a retained record must be rejected and reported as a security event under NFR-11. |
| NFR-11 | The system must emit structured logs, metrics and traces for every credit decision, payment registration, allocation conflict and telemetry gap, and must raise an alert to the on-call channel within 2 minutes when any of these thresholds is crossed: API error rate above 1 % over 5 minutes, decision SLA breaches (FR-04) above 5 per hour, or allocation conflicts above 20 per hour; if the observability backend is unavailable, the system must buffer telemetry locally for 1 hour and must not block or degrade the business transaction it is observing. |
| NFR-12 | Personal and financial data of applicants must be processed in compliance with Peruvian Personal Data Protection Law N.º 29733, restricting credit-dossier access to the Credit Analyst and Risk Supervisor roles defined in NFR-06, honoring a data-access or rectification request within 10 business days, and masking document identifiers and bank references in all logs and non-production environments; if an export of personal data is requested by an unauthorized role it must be blocked and recorded as a security event. |
| NFR-13 | The applicant-facing flows (FR-01, FR-08, FR-12) must be usable on a mobile browser at 360 px width and must meet WCAG 2.1 level AA for contrast, keyboard navigation and form labeling, verified by an automated accessibility scan with zero level-A or level-AA violations on those screens; when the connection drops mid-form, entered data must be preserved locally and restored on the next session rather than lost. |
