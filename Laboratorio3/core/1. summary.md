# Summary

**SendIT** is an international-remittance platform that lets a **Sender** fund money in an
origin currency and lets a **Receiver** obtain an exact, previously confirmed amount in the
local currency of the destination country. The same transaction can be handled directly on
web/mobile or through an **AgencyWorker** at a physical agency for customers who need
assistance.

Before the Sender deposits money, SendIT shows the origin amount, exchange rate, Receiver
amount, sending commission and total to deposit. Once the Sender confirms the quote, the
exchange rate and Receiver amount remain fixed for the entire transaction. SendIT then
tracks funding, security/compliance review, processing and payout. If an eligible transaction
is cancelled before payout, SendIT refunds the funded total minus a small, previously
disclosed operational cancellation fee.

Security, monetary consistency and cross-channel traceability are product guarantees, not
optional implementation details. See [Problem](2.%20problem.md),
[Key product decisions](7.%20key-product-decisions.md) and
[Main flows](9.%20main-flows.md).
