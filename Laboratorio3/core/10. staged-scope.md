# Staged scope

These phases define feasibility and scale for the requirements evaluation. The case study
provides no volume figures; all figures below are **planning assumptions to validate** with
the professor or stakeholders before treating them as contractual.

## Phase 1 — POC / MVP: secure fixed-rate remittance

**Objective:** prove one remittance crosses digital and agency channels end to end while
preserving security and monetary consistency.

**In scope:**

- One enabled origin-to-destination corridor and its two configured currencies.
- Sender responsive web/mobile quote, confirmation and digital funding (Flow 1).
- Agency-assisted Sender quote and cash funding (Flow 2).
- Security/compliance review, processing, status and notifications (Flow 3).
- Receiver cash payout at an agency (Flow 5).
- Pre-payout cancellation/refund with an operational fee (Flow 6).
- Three roles, immutable audit trail, idempotent money commands and shift reconciliation.
- Fixed-amount and percentage sending-commission rules configured in one fee schedule.

**Planning assumptions:** tens of remittances per day, one origin agency and one destination
agency, and up to 10 concurrent authenticated users.

**Committed Happy Path:** Flow 1 → Flow 3 → Flow 5.

## Phase 2 — Multiple corridors and digital payout

**Objective:** expand the payout network without weakening the confirmed monetary snapshot.

**In scope:**

- Multiple enabled country/currency corridors and locale-specific money presentation.
- Digital bank/wallet payout (Flow 4) with provider reconciliation.
- Additional funding providers and agency locations.
- Configurable transaction limits, fee schedules and security/compliance rules by corridor.
- Operational queues, customer support/disputes and enhanced fraud monitoring.

**Planning assumptions:** hundreds of remittances per day, dozens of agencies and up to 100
concurrent authenticated users.

## Phase 3 — Production hardening and network scale

**Objective:** operate a resilient multi-country remittance network.

**In scope:**

- Thousands of remittances per day and hundreds of agencies.
- Provider failover, regional resilience and automated financial reconciliation.
- Advanced fraud analytics, operational reporting and compliance evidence exports.
- Multiple authorized rate, funding and payout providers per corridor.

**Planning assumptions:** at least 10,000 remittances per day and up to 1,000 concurrent
authenticated users.

## Never in scope

- Exchange-rate trading or creation.
- Loans, investments or cryptocurrency trading.
- Anonymous/control-bypassing remittances.
- Changing a confirmed transaction's rate, commission or Receiver amount.
- AgencyWorker ownership of customer decisions.
- Cancellation after payout.

See [Out of scope](4.%20out-of-scope.md) for the authoritative boundary.
