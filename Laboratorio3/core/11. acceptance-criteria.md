# Acceptance criteria

These criteria are the testable baseline behind the title-only backlog. Criteria marked
**planning assumption** come from [Staged scope](10.%20staged-scope.md), not the case study,
and must be validated before becoming contractual.

## AC-1 — Quote and exact conversion

- **AC-1.1** Before confirmation or funding, the quote displays origin amount, origin and
  destination currencies, exchange rate, Receiver amount, sending commission, total to
  deposit and quote expiry.
- **AC-1.2** Receiver amount equals `round_once(origin amount × exchange rate, destination
  currency minor unit)`. For origin amount 100 and rate `x`, the paid amount equals the
  stored result of `100 × x` under that single rounding rule.
- **AC-1.3** Total to deposit equals origin amount plus sending commission; commission is not
  subtracted from Receiver amount.
- **AC-1.4** A fixed fee below the configured threshold and a percentage fee at/above it are
  calculated from the active, versioned fee schedule.
- **AC-1.5** An expired quote cannot be confirmed; requesting a new quote does not modify any
  previously confirmed transaction.

## AC-2 — Monetary snapshot lock

- **AC-2.1** Confirmation persists one immutable monetary snapshot and moves the remittance
  to `AWAITING_FUNDS`.
- **AC-2.2** Market-rate changes, processing delay, retries and movement among web, mobile and
  agency channels do not change the confirmed rate, commission, total or Receiver amount.
- **AC-2.3** Attempts by a user, worker, administrator or provider callback to overwrite a
  confirmed monetary field are rejected and audited.
- **AC-2.4** Payout reads the stored Receiver amount; it never recalculates from a current
  exchange rate.

## AC-3 — Funding

- **AC-3.1** Digital or cash funding succeeds only for the exact total to deposit and creates
  a receipt linked to the remittance.
- **AC-3.2** Reusing a funding idempotency key returns the original result and creates no
  duplicate charge, cash entry or state transition.
- **AC-3.3** Failed or uncertain provider funding remains non-funded or enters reconciliation;
  it is never guessed as successful.
- **AC-3.4** Agency cash funding updates the remittance and shift ledger in one atomic action.

## AC-4 — Review, status and notification

- **AC-4.1** A funded remittance executes the required identity, fraud, limit and compliance
  checks before `READY_FOR_PAYOUT`.
- **AC-4.2** A hold/rejection has a user-safe reason category and next action; sensitive
  detection logic is not exposed.
- **AC-4.3** Every authorized channel displays the same latest persisted state and timeline.
- **AC-4.4** A Receiver notification includes local-currency amount, payout method and safe
  next action while minimizing personal and secret payout data.
- **AC-4.5** A post-funding rejection routes captured funds to the refund path without
  changing the confirmed monetary snapshot.

## AC-5 — Receiver payout

- **AC-5.1** Payout requires `READY_FOR_PAYOUT`, expected Receiver identity and valid bound
  payout authorization/destination.
- **AC-5.2** The amount and currency sent to a provider or shown to an AgencyWorker exactly
  match the stored Receiver amount and destination currency.
- **AC-5.3** A successful payout atomically records `PAID_OUT`, invalidates one-time payout
  authorization and emits one receipt.
- **AC-5.4** Retried commands or callbacks cannot create a second payout.
- **AC-5.5** Amount/currency mismatch, invalid identity, expired authorization or insufficient
  agency cash blocks payout and opens a controlled escalation path.

## AC-6 — Agency-assisted service

- **AC-6.1** Each worker action is attributable to one individual account, agency, shift,
  assisted session and customer consent where applicable.
- **AC-6.2** An AgencyWorker can access only transactions necessary for the assigned agency
  task and cannot view payout secrets or unrelated customer records.
- **AC-6.3** Missing consent or a failed identity/security check blocks the assisted action.
- **AC-6.4** Quotes, statuses and receipts shown/printed at an agency match web/mobile values.
- **AC-6.5** Cash funding, payout and refund totals reconcile to the closed shift ledger;
  mismatches are escalated and never hidden by editing the transaction.

## AC-7 — Cancellation and refund

- **AC-7.1** Before confirmation, SendIT displays funded total, operational cancellation fee
  and exact refund amount.
- **AC-7.2** An eligible cancellation atomically prevents later payout and moves through
  `CANCEL_REQUESTED` to `REFUNDED` after one refund.
- **AC-7.3** A `PAID_OUT` remittance rejects cancellation without charging a cancellation fee.
- **AC-7.4** Concurrent payout and cancellation produce exactly one winner and one monetary
  terminal outcome.
- **AC-7.5** A repeated cancellation/refund command returns the original result and never
  creates a second refund.

## AC-8 — Security, privacy and audit

- **AC-8.1** Role and agency/shift scope are denied by default; every unauthorized attempt is
  rejected without returning transaction data and is audited.
- **AC-8.2** Sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest; payout authorization and
  authentication secrets are never logged in plaintext.
- **AC-8.3** Every quote confirmation, funding, hold, release, payout, cancellation, refund
  and rejected transition creates an append-only audit event.
- **AC-8.4** Security/compliance review cannot alter the confirmed monetary snapshot.
- **AC-8.5** An AgencyWorker cannot approve an exception they initiated or bypass required
  identity evidence.

## AC-9 — Accessibility and localization

- **AC-9.1** Customer web/mobile flows support 360 px width, keyboard-only navigation,
  labelled inputs, visible focus and WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- **AC-9.2** Money, dates, currencies and instructions use the relevant locale without
  changing stored monetary values.
- **AC-9.3** Every critical confirmation names the monetary effect in plain language.
- **AC-9.4** Agency views can show/print the same quote, consent summary and receipts in a
  form the worker can explain without technical terminology.

## AC-10 — POC performance and recovery

- **AC-10.1** **Planning assumption:** at Phase 1 load (tens of daily remittances and 10
  concurrent users), quote, status and transaction-search reads complete within 2 seconds at
  p95, excluding external-provider latency.
- **AC-10.2** Provider timeouts use bounded idempotent retries and then enter reconciliation;
  business transactions are not left in a false terminal state.
- **AC-10.3** **Planning assumption:** Phase 1 monthly availability is at least 99.9%, with
  recovery time objective of 60 minutes and recovery point objective of 5 minutes.
- **AC-10.4** Recovery testing proves that confirmed monetary snapshots, terminal money
  events and audit records are not lost or duplicated.

## POC exit gate

The POC is accepted when Flow 1 → Flow 3 → Flow 5 passes end to end together with applicable
AC-1 through AC-10 checks, and Flow 2 proves the agency-assisted Sender variant uses the same
monetary snapshot and transaction record.
