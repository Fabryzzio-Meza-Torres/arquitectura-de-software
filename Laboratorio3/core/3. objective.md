# Objective

Replace uncertain, fragmented international remittance handling with an experience where:

1. **The money is predictable before payment.** Before any deposit, the Sender sees the
   origin amount, locked exchange rate, exact Receiver amount, sending commission and total
   to deposit.
2. **The promise survives the whole transaction.** Confirmation creates an immutable
   monetary snapshot; processing time, channel changes and market movement never reduce the
   confirmed Receiver amount.
3. **Every release of money is authorized and final only once.** Identity, transaction
   state, payout authorization and role permissions are verified before funding, payout,
   cancellation or refund is recorded.
4. **Digital and assisted service are one coherent product.** A user may start or continue
   on web/mobile or at an agency without duplicating the transaction, changing the price or
   losing traceability.
5. **Cancellation is fair and explicit.** Before an eligible cancellation is confirmed,
   SendIT shows the small operational fee and exact refund; after payout, cancellation is
   prohibited.
6. **No user is left with a black box.** The relevant Sender, Receiver and AgencyWorker can
   see a plain-language status, next action and receipt appropriate to their permissions.
