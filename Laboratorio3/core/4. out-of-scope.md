# Out of scope

The following are not part of SendIT in any delivery phase:

- **Creating or speculating on exchange rates.** SendIT consumes an authorized rate source,
  locks the selected rate and preserves it; it is not a foreign-exchange trading platform.
- **Loans, credit, investments or cryptocurrency trading.** SendIT moves sender-funded
  remittances only.
- **Implementing the external banking and card networks themselves.** SendIT may integrate
  with authorized funding, settlement, bank and wallet providers, but does not replace them.
- **Payout in a currency or destination that SendIT has not enabled.** No silent conversion
  to an alternate currency is permitted.
- **Anonymous or control-bypassing remittances.** Agency assistance does not waive identity,
  security, transaction-limit or compliance checks.
- **AgencyWorker ownership of customer decisions.** A worker may assist and record consent,
  but may not choose the Receiver, accept a quote, cancel or collect funds for a customer.
- **Changing a confirmed transaction's exchange rate, sending commission or Receiver
  amount.** A correction requires cancellation, when eligible, and a new quote.
- **Cancellation after successful payout.** A completed payout is resolved through a
  support/dispute process, never by reversing the paid transaction as if it were unpaid.

A backlog item implementing one of these is scope creep or a contradiction, not additional
coverage.
