# Key product concepts

This vocabulary is authoritative across people, core, requirements and evaluation files.

- **Sender** — person who creates, confirms and funds an international remittance.
- **Receiver** — person authorized to receive the confirmed amount in the destination
  country's local currency.
- **AgencyWorker** — individually authenticated operator who assists customers at an
  assigned SendIT agency and shift without owning their decisions.
- **Remittance quote** — pre-funding price snapshot containing origin amount, origin and
  destination currencies, exchange rate, Receiver amount, sending commission, total to
  deposit and quote expiry.
- **Origin amount** — principal selected by the Sender for conversion; it excludes the
  sending commission.
- **Locked exchange rate** — rate accepted by the Sender. After quote confirmation it is
  immutable for the life of that transaction, including processing delays and channel
  changes.
- **Receiver amount** — exact local-currency payout promised at confirmation:
  `round_once(origin amount × locked exchange rate, destination currency minor unit)`.
  SendIT stores this value in the confirmed monetary snapshot and never recalculates it at
  payout.
- **Sending commission** — charge disclosed before funding. The active fee schedule may use
  a fixed amount for smaller origin amounts and a percentage for larger amounts; the applied
  rule and resulting amount are frozen in the confirmed monetary snapshot.
- **Total to deposit** — `origin amount + sending commission`. For example, an origin amount
  of PEN 100 with a PEN 5 commission requires a PEN 105 deposit.
- **Operational cancellation fee** — small charge for processing an eligible cancellation,
  shown before confirmation of cancellation.
- **Cancellation refund amount** — `funded total − operational cancellation fee`; it is
  shown before the Sender confirms cancellation and is returned through an authorized
  method. When SendIT rejects a funded transaction without a Sender cancellation request,
  the funded total is refunded without the cancellation fee.
- **Confirmed monetary snapshot** — immutable record of origin amount, currencies, locked
  rate, Receiver amount, sending commission, total to deposit, rounding rule and fee-rule
  version accepted by the Sender.
- **Funding** — successful registration and confirmation of the Sender's digital payment or
  agency cash deposit for the exact total to deposit.
- **Payout** — one-time delivery of the stored Receiver amount through a supported digital
  method or cash at an agency.
- **Payout authorization** — one-time credential and identity check required before payout;
  it becomes unusable immediately after successful payout.
- **Assisted session** — time-bounded record linking a customer, AgencyWorker, agency, shift,
  consent and actions performed on the customer's behalf.
- **Security/compliance review** — controlled state for identity, fraud, transaction-limit
  or legally required checks. A hold does not change the monetary snapshot.
- **Idempotency** — processing the same confirmed command more than once produces one
  business effect and returns the original result.
- **Agency shift ledger** — worker/agency record of cash funding, payout and refund events
  used for shift reconciliation.
- **Receipt** — immutable customer-facing evidence of funding, payout, cancellation or
  refund, with sensitive values minimized.
- **Audit event** — append-only record of actor, role, channel, agency when applicable,
  timestamp, action, transaction, prior state and new state.

## Remittance state machine

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `DRAFT` | Sender or AgencyWorker is capturing data; no price is promised. |
| `QUOTED` | A quote is displayed but has not been confirmed. |
| `AWAITING_FUNDS` | Sender confirmed the monetary snapshot; exact funding is pending. |
| `FUNDED` | Exact total to deposit was registered once. |
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | Security/compliance checks require processing or intervention. |
| `PROCESSING` | Authorized cross-border processing is underway. |
| `READY_FOR_PAYOUT` | Stored Receiver amount may be released through the authorized method. |
| `PAID_OUT` | Receiver amount was delivered once; terminal and not cancellable. |
| `CANCEL_REQUESTED` | Eligible pre-payout cancellation and refund are being processed. |
| `REFUNDED` | Funded total minus operational cancellation fee was returned; terminal. |
| `REJECTED` | Transaction cannot proceed; any captured funds follow the refund path. |

No channel keeps a separate state machine. Every transition is validated and audited.
