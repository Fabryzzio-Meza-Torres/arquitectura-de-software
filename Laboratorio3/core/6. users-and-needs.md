# Users and their needs

Three roles use SendIT. The detailed personas live in `people/*.md`; this section is the
role-based checklist used by `agents/eval-spec.md`.

## Sender

Initiates, confirms and funds the remittance, directly or with agency assistance.

**Needs:** secure access and identity protection · unambiguous Receiver and payout data · a
complete pre-funding quote · an exchange rate and Receiver amount fixed throughout the
transaction · digital or agency funding with proof · current status and timeline · reasons
and next steps for holds/rejections/failures · eligible cancellation with fee and refund
shown first · payout/refund receipts · assisted service under explicit consent.

**Pain points:** hidden fees · rate changes after deposit · fraud or duplicate charges ·
unclear status · difficult forms · unknown cancellation deductions.

## Receiver

Receives exactly the confirmed local-currency amount through the authorized payout method.

**Needs:** safe notification and incoming-transfer status · secure identity and payout
authorization · exact local-currency payout with no deduction · supported digital or agency
payout · protected cash pickup · proof of one-time payout · clear exception/support path ·
accessible, localized or assisted service.

**Pain points:** receiving less than promised · impersonation · unclear collection
instructions · vague status · interfaces that assume high digital literacy.

## AgencyWorker

Provides assisted service at a physical agency without replacing customer decisions or
security controls.

**Needs:** individual, agency- and shift-scoped access · privacy-preserving transaction
search · assisted Sender flow with recorded consent · printable quote before deposit ·
idempotent cash funding and receipt · Receiver identity and one-time authorization checks ·
stored payout amount with no manual calculation · one-time cash payout · controlled
cancellation/refund assistance · exception escalation · shift reconciliation.

**Pain points:** manual money calculations · shared credentials · pressure to bypass checks ·
duplicate posting after retries · channel inconsistencies · screens that are hard to explain.
