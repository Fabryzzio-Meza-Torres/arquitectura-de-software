# Key product decisions

These decisions are settled constraints for SendIT. A backlog item that contradicts one is a
defect.

## KPD-1 — Three roles and three channels share one remittance record

The authenticated human roles are **Sender**, **Receiver** when an account is used, and
**AgencyWorker**. Web, mobile-responsive web and physical agency service are channels over
the same remittance, state machine and audit timeline; moving between channels never creates
a second transaction or a second price.

## KPD-2 — Confirmation locks the monetary snapshot for the whole transaction

Before confirmation, a displayed quote may expire and be replaced. Confirmation freezes the
origin amount, currencies, exchange rate, Receiver amount, sending commission, total to
deposit, rounding result and fee-rule version. Funding, review, processing delay, channel
change and payout never recalculate that snapshot.

For an origin amount of 100 and a locked rate `x`, the stored Receiver amount is exactly
`round_once(100 × x, destination currency minor unit)`, and that stored amount—not a later
market calculation—is paid.

## KPD-3 — Every sending charge is disclosed before deposit

The quote separates principal from commission and shows the total to deposit before the
Sender commits funds. The active fee schedule may define a fixed commission below a
configured threshold and a percentage commission at or above it. The threshold, rule and
amount are versioned; no sending charge may be added after funding.

## KPD-4 — The Receiver obtains the confirmed amount without a payout deduction

SendIT pays the stored Receiver amount in the destination local currency. A payout partner
or agency may not subtract a SendIT commission from it. If the exact amount cannot be
delivered, the payout is stopped and escalated rather than silently reduced or converted.

## KPD-5 — Cancellation is pre-payout, explicit and fee-bearing

An eligible funded remittance may be cancelled until payout wins the final atomic state
transition. Before cancellation, the Sender sees the operational cancellation fee and exact
refund. Confirmation moves the transaction to the cancellation/refund path; the refund is
the funded total minus that fee. A `PAID_OUT` transaction cannot be cancelled.

## KPD-6 — Security controls apply equally to self-service and assisted service

Identity verification, authentication, transaction limits, fraud/compliance checks,
encryption, least privilege and auditability apply to every channel. Agency presence is not
a reason to weaken a control. A security hold preserves the monetary snapshot and gives the
authorized user a plain-language status and next step without disclosing sensitive detection
logic.

## KPD-7 — The AgencyWorker assists; the customer decides

Every AgencyWorker uses an individual account scoped to an agency and shift. Actions on a
customer's behalf require an assisted session and recorded consent. The worker may explain
and capture, but may not choose a Receiver, accept a quote, bypass identity checks, change a
confirmed amount, or access unrelated transactions.

## KPD-8 — Money-moving commands are idempotent and state transitions are atomic

Quote confirmation, funding, payout, cancellation and refund each accept an idempotency key.
Retries return the original result. Competing payout and cancellation commands are serialized
so exactly one terminal path succeeds. Every accepted or rejected transition leaves an audit
event.

## KPD-9 — Payout authorization is one-time and bound to the transaction

A Receiver's payout requires the expected identity evidence and a one-time authorization
bound to the remittance and payout method. It is stored securely, expires under the active
security policy and becomes unusable immediately after payout. An AgencyWorker cannot view
or override the secret value.

## KPD-10 — Accessibility and localization are part of the core experience

Sender and Receiver information is presented in plain language, local currency and locale-
appropriate formats. Web/mobile flows meet the declared accessibility standard; agencies
provide the same quote and receipts in a printable, explainable form. No user must understand
exchange-rate mathematics to know what they pay and receive.

## KPD-11 — External providers never become the monetary source of truth

Funding, rate and payout providers may report events, but SendIT owns the confirmed monetary
snapshot and transaction state. Provider retries or conflicting callbacks cannot overwrite a
terminal state or change the promised amount; they enter a controlled reconciliation queue.
