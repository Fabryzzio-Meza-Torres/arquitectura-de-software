# Expected user experience

This section defines how SendIT should feel and which guarantees users perceive. The detailed
steps live in [Main flows](9.%20main-flows.md).

## Principles for every role and channel

- **Price before payment.** No deposit button or cash acceptance appears before the complete
  quote and total are shown.
- **One promise.** The exchange rate and Receiver amount visible at confirmation remain the
  same everywhere until payout or refund.
- **One current status.** Web, mobile and agency screens read the same state and timeline.
- **Security with an explanation.** A hold stops unsafe movement and gives a safe next step;
  it does not make the transaction disappear.
- **No duplicate money movement.** Repeating a click, callback or worker action returns the
  prior result.
- **Plain language.** The interface says “You deposit”, “Commission”, “Receiver gets” and
  “Refund”, with local currency symbols and no unexplained technical jargon.

## Sender

The Sender should be able to:

1. Enter Receiver, destination and origin amount in a short guided flow.
2. Review a single breakdown of origin amount, exchange rate, Receiver amount, commission
   and total to deposit before confirming.
3. Fund digitally or hand the exact total to an AgencyWorker and receive a receipt.
4. Track the same remittance across channels without calling support for a status.
5. See why action is needed when a transfer is held, rejected or fails.
6. Preview the cancellation fee and exact refund before cancelling an eligible transfer.
7. Obtain immutable proof of payout or refund.

The Sender should never discover a late charge, receive a different rate after funding, or
lose control because a worker is helping.

## Receiver

The Receiver should be able to:

1. Receive a safe, localized notification with the amount and next step.
2. Verify identity without exposing more information than necessary.
3. Receive the exact confirmed local-currency amount digitally or as agency cash.
4. Obtain a receipt and know that the payout authorization can no longer be reused.
5. See a clear support path when payout cannot proceed.

The Receiver should never be asked to absorb a SendIT commission at payout or perform an
unannounced second conversion.

## AgencyWorker

The AgencyWorker experience is a guided operational checklist:

1. Sign in individually and open an assigned shift.
2. Find only the relevant transaction or start an assisted session with recorded consent.
3. Show/print the same complete quote the digital channel displays.
4. Register cash funding, payout or refund without manual exchange-rate arithmetic.
5. Stop automatically when identity, authorization, cash amount or state is invalid.
6. Issue a receipt and reconcile the shift ledger.

The worker should never need shared credentials, side spreadsheets or an override that
bypasses a security or monetary invariant.

## Experience quality bar

- A user can explain the complete price before funding.
- The confirmed Receiver amount is visually prominent and unchanged throughout processing.
- Critical actions require an explicit confirmation summarizing their monetary effect.
- Every screen has one primary next action and an accessible help/escalation path.
- An older adult can complete the process with AgencyWorker assistance without surrendering
  decision ownership.
