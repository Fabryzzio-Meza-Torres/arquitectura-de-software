# Main flows

These are the end-to-end flows used by the coverage gate in `agents/eval-spec.md`.

Legend: **[S]** Sender · **[R]** Receiver · **[AW]** AgencyWorker · **[SYS]** SendIT.

## Flow 1 — Direct quote, confirmation and funding

**Goal:** a Sender uses web/mobile to create and fund a remittance with a fixed promise.

1. **[S]** Authenticates and provides Receiver, destination, payout method and origin amount.
2. **[SYS]** verifies that the corridor, currency, payout method and transaction are allowed.
3. **[SYS]** shows origin amount, exchange rate, exact Receiver amount, sending commission,
   total to deposit and quote expiry.
4. **[S]** explicitly confirms the quote.
5. **[SYS]** stores the immutable monetary snapshot and moves to `AWAITING_FUNDS`.
6. **[S]** funds the exact total through a supported digital method.
7. **[SYS]** registers funding once, issues a receipt and continues to Flow 3.

**Edge cases / fallbacks:** an expired unconfirmed quote must be refreshed; invalid Receiver
data blocks confirmation; repeated confirmation or funding returns the prior result; a failed
funding attempt does not mark the remittance funded.

## Flow 2 — Agency-assisted quote and cash funding

**Goal:** a customer completes the Sender flow at a physical agency without losing control.

1. **[AW]** authenticates, opens the assigned shift and starts an assisted session.
2. **[S]** provides identity, Receiver and transaction information; **[AW]** records consent.
3. **[SYS]** produces the same quote and validations as Flow 1.
4. **[AW]** shows or prints the complete quote; **[S]** confirms it.
5. **[AW]** counts and registers the exact cash total to deposit.
6. **[SYS]** posts funding once to the remittance and agency shift ledger and issues receipts.
7. **[SYS]** continues to Flow 3 with the same monetary snapshot.

**Edge cases / fallbacks:** wrong cash total blocks posting; missing consent or failed identity
blocks confirmation; connectivity retry cannot duplicate the cash entry; drawer mismatch is
escalated and does not alter the customer quote.

## Flow 3 — Review, processing and tracking

**Goal:** move an authorized funded remittance toward payout without changing its promise.

1. **[SYS]** runs identity, fraud, limit and required compliance checks.
2. **[SYS]** moves the remittance through `UNDER_REVIEW` when intervention is needed, then
   `PROCESSING` when authorized.
3. **[S]** sees status, timeline and a safe explanation/next step for any hold or rejection.
4. **[SYS]** prepares the stored Receiver amount through the authorized payout method.
5. **[SYS]** moves to `READY_FOR_PAYOUT` and safely notifies **[R]** of amount and next step.

**Edge cases / fallbacks:** provider timeout is retried idempotently and then reconciled; a
rejection after funding routes captured money to the refund path; no hold or retry changes
rate, commission or Receiver amount.

## Flow 4 — Digital payout to the Receiver

**Goal:** deliver the exact Receiver amount to an authorized bank/wallet destination.

1. **[SYS]** validates remittance state, Receiver identity and bound payout destination.
2. **[SYS]** sends the stored Receiver amount in destination local currency.
3. **[SYS]** accepts a successful provider result only once and moves to `PAID_OUT`.
4. **[S]** and **[R]** receive status and receipt appropriate to their permissions.

**Edge cases / fallbacks:** a mismatch between provider amount/currency and the monetary
snapshot blocks payout; duplicate callbacks return the recorded result; an uncertain result
stays in reconciliation and never triggers a second payment.

## Flow 5 — Cash payout at an agency

**Goal:** an authorized Receiver collects the exact amount in local cash.

1. **[AW]** authenticates and locates the remittance with privacy-preserving search.
2. **[R]** presents the required identity evidence and one-time payout authorization.
3. **[SYS]** validates identity, authorization, `READY_FOR_PAYOUT` state and agency cash
   availability, then shows the stored Receiver amount.
4. **[AW]** counts the exact local-currency cash; **[R]** confirms receipt.
5. **[SYS]** atomically moves to `PAID_OUT`, invalidates the authorization, updates the shift
   ledger and issues receipts.

**Edge cases / fallbacks:** invalid/expired authorization, identity mismatch or insufficient
cash blocks payout and gives an escalation path; a retry cannot pay twice; the worker cannot
edit the payout amount.

## Flow 6 — Cancellation and refund before payout

**Goal:** cancel an eligible funded remittance with a transparent operational charge.

1. **[S]** or an explicitly authorized assisted session requests cancellation.
2. **[SYS]** verifies that payout has not completed and that the transaction is currently
   eligible, without yet changing its state.
3. **[SYS]** shows funded total, operational cancellation fee and exact refund amount.
4. **[S]** confirms cancellation.
5. **[SYS]** atomically reserves the cancellation path against any competing payout, moves to
   `CANCEL_REQUESTED`, processes the refund once and then records `REFUNDED` with a receipt.

**Edge cases / fallbacks:** `PAID_OUT` is not cancellable; if payout wins the concurrent
transition, cancellation fails without charging a fee; a refund-provider timeout enters
reconciliation and is not submitted a second time blindly.

## Flow 7 — Security or operational exception

**Goal:** resolve suspicious, mismatched or technically uncertain activity safely.

1. **[SYS]** places the transaction or action on hold without changing its monetary snapshot.
2. **[SYS]** records reason category and audit evidence, restricting sensitive detection data.
3. **[S]**, **[R]** or **[AW]** sees the permitted status and required next action.
4. An authorized reviewer resolves the hold as resume, reject/refund or support escalation.
5. **[SYS]** records and notifies the outcome.

**Edge cases / fallbacks:** an AgencyWorker cannot self-approve an exception they initiated;
an unresolved provider callback cannot be guessed as success; audit evidence remains
immutable.

> **POC Happy Path:** Flow 1 → Flow 3 → Flow 5 is implemented end to end. Flow 2 demonstrates
> the assisted Sender variant against the same transaction model.
