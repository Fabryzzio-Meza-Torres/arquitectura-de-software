# Sender — Fabryzzio

## Who they are

Fabryzzio represents the **Sender**: the person who initiates an international remittance and
provides the money that SendIT must deliver to a Receiver in the Receiver's local currency.
The Sender may use SendIT directly from the web or a mobile device, or may ask an
AgencyWorker for assisted service at a physical agency.

## Role in the system

The Sender owns the remittance instruction. They choose the Receiver, origin amount,
destination country and payout method; review the exchange rate and every fee; confirm the
transaction; fund the total amount; track delivery; and, while payout is still reversible,
request cancellation.

An AgencyWorker may guide or operate the flow, but may never confirm a transaction, change a
Receiver, or accept a quote without the Sender's explicit consent.

## Goals

- Send money abroad safely without hidden costs.
- Know exactly how much to deposit before paying.
- Guarantee the exact amount the Receiver will obtain in local currency.
- Track the remittance until payout or refund.
- Use a physical agency when the digital channel is difficult or unavailable.

## Needs from the system

- Securely identify themselves and protect access to their transactions.
- Register the Receiver and the destination payout method without ambiguity.
- Receive, before funding, a quote showing origin amount, locked exchange rate, Receiver
  amount, sending commission and total amount to deposit.
- Be certain that the locked exchange rate and Receiver amount will not change after
  confirmation, regardless of how long processing takes.
- Fund through an available digital method or with cash at an agency and receive proof.
- See a clear, current status and a traceable timeline for the remittance.
- Receive an understandable explanation if the remittance is held, rejected or fails.
- Cancel before payout, first seeing the operational cancellation fee and exact refund.
- Obtain a receipt for payout or cancellation and refund.
- Complete the same safe flow with AgencyWorker assistance when needed.

## Pain points

- Hidden commissions discovered only after starting payment.
- A changing exchange rate that causes the Receiver to obtain less than promised.
- Fear of fraud, identity theft, duplicate charges or sending to the wrong person.
- Unclear statuses and no proof that the Receiver was paid.
- Complex forms, technical language or poor mobile usability.
- Not knowing how much will be retained when cancelling.

## Main flows this role participates in

1. Request and confirm a remittance quote.
2. Fund the remittance digitally or at an agency.
3. Track processing and payout.
4. Cancel an eligible remittance and receive the refund minus the disclosed operational fee.
5. Complete any of the above through an AgencyWorker with explicit consent.

## What this role expects from SendIT

- One consistent transaction and price across web, mobile and agency channels.
- Plain-language money breakdowns before every confirmation.
- No silent changes to the exchange rate, fees, Receiver or payout amount.
- Strong authentication, visible receipts and prompt security alerts.
- Assisted service that remains under the Sender's control.

## Success criteria

- Before depositing, the Sender can state the total charge and the exact Receiver amount.
- The funded remittance pays exactly the confirmed Receiver amount in local currency.
- A retry never creates a duplicate remittance or duplicate charge.
- The Sender can always prove whether the transaction was paid, cancelled or refunded.
