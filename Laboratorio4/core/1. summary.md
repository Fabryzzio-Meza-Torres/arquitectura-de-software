# Summary

**RemoteSchooly** is the Government of Peru initiative for delivering official Regular Basic
Education material to rural schools with limited internet connectivity. **Balbuena
EduKanvas** is the platform used to prepare, distribute and consume that material.

Government Teachers in Lima prepare one official package for each two-month academic
period. The package contains curriculum, teacher guides, documents, printable activities,
images and videos. It travels through the cloud to departmental servers and then to school
servers. After integrity verification, the school activates one complete version and serves
it to teachers and students over its LAN throughout the period.

Rural Teachers use the package to plan and conduct classes, publish tasks and announcements,
review submissions, provide feedback and record grades. Rural Students use assigned XO
laptops to access enrolled courses, submit permitted files and view their own results. The
platform has no student forums or student-authored comments.

The second product concern is the high token cost of AI-assisted content creation. The
solution must reduce token consumption by at least 40% against a comparable baseline while
preserving the required educational output.
