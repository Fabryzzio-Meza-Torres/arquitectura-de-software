# Staged scope

The case study provides no national school, user or file-volume figures. No unsupported
capacity target is treated as contractual. The known school-level constraint is 12 physical
classrooms operating in parallel.

## Phase 1 — Central preparation and controlled AI

**Objective:** produce one identifiable official package for a two-month period.

**In scope:**

- Authentication and authorization by role.
- Academic structure preconfigured by a technical account.
- Package creation, upload, preview, versioning and publication.
- AI assistant with usage records, configurable limits and the 40% token-reduction target.

## Phase 2 — Verified rural distribution

**Objective:** deliver one complete package from Lima to each school without exposing partial
content.

**In scope:**

- Lima, cloud, departmental-server and school-server route.
- Large-file retry or resume, manifest verification and atomic activation.
- Temporary-cache removal only after confirmed synchronization.
- LAN access to the active package throughout the period.

## Phase 3 — Local learning experience

**Objective:** support planning, classroom delivery and essential academic work locally.

**In scope:**

- Teacher review, playback, download and printing of material.
- Student access from XO laptops.
- Tasks, announcements, submissions, grades, feedback and period-grade records.
- Twelve physical classrooms using the school server in parallel.

## Never in scope

- Ministry of Education system integration or grade export.
- A 100% availability guarantee or advanced high availability.
- Student forums or social communication.
- Built-in collaborative document editing.
- Rural Teacher creation of official curriculum or academic structure.

See [Out of scope](4.%20out-of-scope.md) for the authoritative boundary.
