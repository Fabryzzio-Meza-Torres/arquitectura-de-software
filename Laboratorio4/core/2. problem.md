# Problem

Peru's Ministry of Education must deliver a complete official package for every two-month
academic period to rural schools across the country. Content originates in Lima, while the
destinations have limited internet access and cannot depend on direct downloads during
class. The proposed route is Lima, cloud, departmental server, school server and school LAN.

## 1. Interrupted distribution can leave rural schools with incomplete material

The package includes large and varied files. A network interruption between Lima, a
department or a school can leave partial or corrupt content. Activating that content would
cause different classrooms to use inconsistent versions or lose access to required
documents and videos.

The system must resume interrupted transfers, verify every file against the package manifest
and activate only one complete version. Previously active content must remain available
until the replacement passes verification.

## 2. Limited connectivity and digital skills threaten the two-month learning cycle

Each school uses a planning week to review the complete package before the period begins.
During the period, teachers and students need local access even when external internet is
unavailable. A school may use 12 physical classrooms in parallel, with primary classes in
the morning and secondary classes reusing the rooms in the afternoon.

Rural Teachers have basic digital skills and receive training to configure their school's
courses, grades, classrooms, groups and enrollments, in addition to using clear flows for
material, tasks, submissions, feedback and grades. Rural Students use low-resource XO
laptops and must see only their enrolled courses and personal results.

## 3. AI-assisted content creation consumes too many tokens

Government Teachers use an AI assistant to create and improve educational material. Current
usage creates excessive token cost. The platform must reduce consumption by at least 40%
against a repeatable, comparable baseline without omitting required educational outputs.

Official grade registration in Ministry of Education systems is manual and external.
Balbuena EduKanvas does not export grades or integrate with those systems in this scope.
