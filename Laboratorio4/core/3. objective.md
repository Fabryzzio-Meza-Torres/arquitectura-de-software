# Objective

Design Balbuena EduKanvas so each rural school receives one complete official package before
the two-month academic period begins, serves it locally during classes and manages essential
learning activities without depending on external internet access.

The product must let Government Teachers prepare, version and publish official material;
move the package through cloud, departmental and school infrastructure; verify integrity
before activation; and keep the active version available over the school LAN. Rural Teachers
must be able to plan classes, use material, manage tasks and record grades. Rural Students
must access only their enrolled courses, submit allowed work and view their own results.

Success also requires at least 40% lower AI-token consumption against the agreed comparable
baseline, automatic recovery of interrupted large-file transfers, safe temporary-cache
cleanup after confirmed synchronization and support for 12 classrooms operating in parallel.
