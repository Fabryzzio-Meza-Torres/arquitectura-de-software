# Out of scope

- Integration, export or automatic grade entry into Ministry of Education systems.
- A 100% availability guarantee.
- Advanced high availability, disaster recovery or multi-region replication.
- Direct internet connectivity from every classroom laptop or XO device.
- Course, classroom, group or enrollment creation by Rural Teachers; a secondary technical
  account performs that configuration.
- Creation of official curriculum or independent digital curriculum by Rural Teachers.
- Detailed student profiles; teaching records require only full name, enrollment identifier
  and relevant academic data.
- Student forums, social chat or student-authored comments.
- Live streaming of classes or videos.
- Collaborative document editing inside the platform; editable files open in compatible
  applications.
- Public courses unrelated to the student's enrollment, grade or classroom.
