# Users and their needs

The three model users are detailed in [`people/`](../people/). This file is the condensed
role-level view used by the product specification.

## Government Teacher

- Authenticate with an authorized central account.
- Organize and publish an official package by period, course, grade and classroom.
- Upload and preview every required educational format.
- Create or improve material with an AI assistant under visible token controls.
- Identify the active package version and distribution status for every destination.
- Correct published material through a new version without replacing usable content with a
  partial package.

## Rural Teacher

- Authenticate and see only assigned courses, classrooms and students.
- After training, configure courses, grades, classrooms, groups, enrollments and teacher
  assignments for the school.
- Review the complete package during the planning week.
- View, download, play and print active material over the LAN without external internet.
- Add complementary official material without changing the central curriculum.
- Create and schedule tasks and announcements.
- Review submissions, assign grades, leave feedback and record period grades.
- Receive understandable version, synchronization and pending-work notices.

## Rural Student

- Sign in with teacher assistance when needed and access only the current enrollment.
- View or download period material over the LAN from an XO laptop.
- Open editable files in a compatible application.
- Review tasks, submit allowed files and view personal grades and teacher feedback.
- Learn through in-person interaction without forums or student-authored comments.
