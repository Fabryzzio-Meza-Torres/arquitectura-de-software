# Expected user experience

## Principles for every role

- Show the current academic period, package version and relevant status without requiring
  technical interpretation.
- Preserve usable active content while a replacement is downloading or being verified.
- Expose only actions and records permitted by role, assignment and enrollment.
- Use direct labels and short instructions suitable for users with limited digital skills.

## Government Teacher

After sign-in, Melchor sees the periods and packages under his responsibility. He creates or
edits material, checks AI-token consumption, previews files and publishes a version. A
destination view reports each department and school as pending, synchronizing, verified,
active or failed, with an actionable explanation for failures.

## Rural Teacher

After sign-in, Gaspar can configure the school's academic structure and enrollments using
the training-guided administration flow, then lands on assigned classrooms, the current
period and the active package. During planning week he reviews the complete content
inventory. During class he opens, plays or prints material without external internet. Task,
submission and grading screens use student full names, short instructions and visible next
actions.

## Rural Student

After sign-in, Baltasar sees only enrolled courses. Each course shows current material and
tasks. Files state whether they can be viewed, downloaded or opened in another application.
He may submit allowed files and read personal grades and feedback. No forum or comment
control is displayed.

## Experience quality bar

- Loss of external internet does not remove already active material from the LAN.
- Interrupted synchronization leaves the active version available and shows the replacement
  as retrying or failed.
- A new file is never shown as usable before integrity verification.
- A trained Rural Teacher can configure the school's academic structure and complete the
  core classroom flow without technical assistance.
