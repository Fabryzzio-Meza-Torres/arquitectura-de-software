# Rural Student — Baltasar

## Who they are

Baltasar represents a **Rural Student** enrolled between first grade of primary school and
fifth grade of secondary school in Peru's Regular Basic Education system. He uses an
assigned low-resource XO laptop and may receive teacher assistance when signing in.

## Role in the system

Baltasar attends class, signs in to Balbuena EduKanvas over the school LAN, opens material
for his enrolled courses, reviews tasks, submits files when required and consults his own
grades and teacher feedback. He may open editable files in a compatible application.

Questions are handled in person. Baltasar does not participate in forums or publish
comments through the platform.

## Goals

- Access each course's material during class.
- View or download locally available documents, PDFs, images and videos.
- Open editable documents in a compatible application when required.
- Review tasks, submit files and consult grades and feedback.
- Use printable material or copy exercises into a notebook when instructed.

## Needs from the system

- Sign in, with teacher assistance when needed, and access only his enrollment.
- See the current two-month material organized by course and activity.
- View or download material over the LAN without external internet.
- Open editable files using a compatible application installed on the XO laptop.
- Review task instructions and attach files when the task accepts a digital submission.
- View only his own grades and the teacher's feedback.

## Pain points

- Limited XO laptop resources.
- Little or no external internet connectivity.
- Different digital-literacy levels across ages and grades.
- Risk of exposure to another student's courses, submissions or grades.

## Main flows this role participates in

1. Sign in with optional teacher assistance.
2. Open or download material for an enrolled course.
3. Review a task and submit an allowed file.
4. View a personal grade and teacher feedback.

## What this role expects from Balbuena EduKanvas

- Course material works over the school LAN without external internet.
- Navigation exposes only the current enrollment and age-appropriate actions.
- Files clearly indicate whether they can be viewed, downloaded or opened elsewhere.
- No forum or comment controls distract from the in-person classroom experience.

## Success criteria

- Baltasar accesses assigned material from the XO laptop during an internet outage.
- He can submit an allowed file and later see its grade and feedback.
- He cannot access another student's records or publish comments.
