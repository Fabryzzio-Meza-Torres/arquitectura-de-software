# Government Teacher — Melchor

## Who they are

Melchor represents a **Government Teacher** who works closely with Peru's Ministry of
Education in Lima. Together with colleagues in the same role, he defines the syllabus,
curriculum and official learning materials for compulsory Regular Basic Education.

## Role in the system

Melchor prepares one official package for each two-month academic period. He organizes
content for preconfigured courses, grades and classrooms; uploads or creates the required
files; may use the Balbuena EduKanvas AI assistant; reviews the package; publishes a
version; and monitors its distribution through departmental and school servers.

Official grade entry in the Ministry of Education system remains a separate manual process
and is not performed through Balbuena EduKanvas.

## Goals

- Complete and publish the official package before each school's two-month planning week.
- Deliver one complete, traceable version to departmental and school servers.
- Produce PDFs, informational documents, teacher guides, digital books, worksheets,
  activities, infographics, images and videos.
- Reduce AI token consumption by at least 40% without omitting required learning materials.

## Needs from the system

- Sign in with an authorized Government Teacher account.
- Create and organize material by academic period, course, grade and target classroom.
- Upload and preview every supported educational format before publication.
- Use an AI assistant that selects appropriate skills and models, observes configured token
  limits and explains when a limit has been reached.
- Publish an identifiable package version and see its distribution status by destination.
- Correct a published package through a new version without confusing it with the active
  version in rural schools.

## Pain points

- Current AI-assisted content creation consumes too many tokens.
- An incomplete or corrupt publication can leave a school without material for the period.
- Connectivity between Lima, departments and schools can be interrupted.
- Ambiguous versions can cause teachers to plan lessons using obsolete content.

## Main flows this role participates in

1. Sign in and prepare a two-month material package.
2. Create or improve material with the controlled AI assistant.
3. Preview and publish a version with its file manifest.
4. Monitor distribution and publish a corrected version when necessary.

## What this role expects from Balbuena EduKanvas

- Clear organization by period, course, grade and classroom.
- Visible AI-token consumption and enforceable limits.
- Unambiguous package versions and destination status.
- No activation of incomplete material at a rural school.

## Success criteria

- Melchor publishes a complete package before the planning week.
- Every destination reports either a verified active version or an actionable synchronization
  error.
- A corrected package never silently overwrites the active version.
- The agreed benchmark demonstrates at least 40% lower AI-token consumption.
