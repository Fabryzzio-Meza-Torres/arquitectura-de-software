# Engineering Manager — Jesús Bellido

## Who they are

Jesús represents the **Head of the Engineering Area** at Balbuena Consulting Enterprise. He
doesn't resolve technical tickets day to day, but he is the entry point for every
escalation the Harness routes to his area. His role is to make sure each escalated incident
reaches the right engineer on his team, that business SLAs are met, and that severe
incidents — like the accidental deletion of a database — never happen again.

## Role in the system

Jesús receives, via Slack (MCP), notifications for tickets that the Harness couldn't
resolve and that were escalated from Support or generated by his own area. His job is to
route each ticket to his team (for example, to Julia Saenz) idempotently — meaning the same
escalation must never generate multiple notifications or duplicate assignments. He is also
ultimately responsible for his team meeting the 3-day SLA for Engineering Escalations and
the 1-day SLA for Customer Escalations that reach engineering.

## Goals

- Have full visibility into which incidents are escalated and who they were assigned to.
- Avoid bottlenecks: no ticket should go unassigned due to duplicate or lost notifications.
- Guarantee his team meets business SLAs (1 day for customers, 3 days for engineering).
- Prevent high-risk incidents (like an accidental database wipe) that damage client trust
  and Balbuena's reputation.
- Understand why the LLM is failing or giving inconsistent answers, so he can push for
  improvements to the harness.

## Needs from the system

- A reliable notification channel (MCP to Slack) that guarantees each escalated ticket
  arrives exactly once, to the right person or team.
- Clear traceability of why a ticket was escalated (what the Harness tried, which guardrail
  triggered, whether a risky action was blocked).
- Visibility into SLA compliance metrics by incident type (Customer vs. Engineering
  Escalation).
- Confidence that the system has guardrails that block destructive LLM actions (e.g.,
  deleting a database) until explicit human confirmation is given.
- Reports or alerts when incident volume spikes (e.g., the first week of the month) so he
  can reinforce his team in time.

## Pain points

- The past incident where the LLM, following instructions from a support engineer, decided
  to delete a database without any control or confirmation.
- Inconsistency in the LLM: the same questions get different answers day to day.
- The LLM wasn't learning from resolved tickets, generating repeated work for his team.
- Risk of duplicate Slack notifications when the same ticket is retried or reprocessed
  without idempotency control.
- Spikes of more than 10,000 incidents per week that can overload both the LLM and his
  team's response capacity.

## Main flows this role participates in

1. Receive the notification for an escalated ticket via Slack (MCP).
2. Route the ticket to the corresponding engineer on his team, idempotently.
3. Track SLA compliance for tickets escalated to his area.
4. Get informed whenever the Harness blocked a sensitive action (guardrail) that requires a
   human decision.

## What this role expects from Genius-x (Harness)

- Reliable guardrails that prevent any destructive or irreversible action without explicit
  human approval.
- Idempotent escalation via Slack: no duplicate notifications, no ticket assigned twice.
- A priority queue system that ensures the most urgent incidents (Customer Escalations) are
  handled first, even during load spikes.
- Consistency in the LLM's answers to repeated questions, thanks to caching and a RAG that
  actually learns from each interaction.
- Resilience to failure: retries, timeouts, and fallback so the system is never completely
  unresponsive to a client or his team.

## Success criteria

- No incident ever causes an unapproved destructive action (like deleting a database)
  again.
- 100% of escalations reach Slack exactly once and are assigned unambiguously.
- His team meets the 3-day SLA for Engineering Escalations and the 1-day SLA for Customer
  Escalations, even during the first week of the month.
- The Harness shows measurable improvement in answer consistency thanks to nightly RAG
  retraining.
