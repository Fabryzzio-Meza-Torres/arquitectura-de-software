# 1. Summary

**Genius-x** is the incident-management Harness for Balbuena Consulting Enterprise, an ERP provider for domestic and international clients. It helps support and engineering register, investigate, reassess, reopen, and escalate tickets through an existing local LLM, RAG, and controlled tools. Many reports are ERP user errors; the system must help support resolve them without consuming engineering capacity unnecessarily.

The Harness never performs a production solution or closes a ticket based on an LLM decision. A person reviews the proposal, performs the solution, and records the outcome. The architecture reference is [`../diagram/final_architecture.excalidraw`](../diagram/final_architecture.excalidraw); the study case and clarifications are in [`../study-case/contexto.md`](../study-case/contexto.md).
