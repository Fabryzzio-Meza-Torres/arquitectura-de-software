# 10. Staged scope

| Stage | Verifiable scope |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Tickets and human control | Registration, types and deadlines, statuses and history, provided credentials, proposals without execution, human review and closure, reopening. |
| 2. Escalation and reliability | Prioritization, idempotency, Slack with deduplication, human assignment, limits, timeout/retry/circuit breaker, fallback, and dead-letter queue. |
| 3. Answer quality | RAG from validated sources, nightly index update, cache and invalidation, isolated sandbox, workload metrics, and compliance tracking. |

Stage 1 demonstrates F1 and the prohibition of autonomous action; Stage 2 completes F2, F3, and the failure route; Stage 3 improves consistency without changing human authority. The final design must represent the three stages and the components of [`../diagram/final_architecture.excalidraw`](../diagram/final_architecture.excalidraw).
