# 2. Problem

1. **Overload and poor classification.** Support receives many client reports, including simple ERP user errors; difficult cases must reach engineering with the correct context and priority.
2. **Unsafe or unreliable answers.** The previous LLM could decide to delete a database, repeats mistakes, gives different answers to common questions, and displays ticket statuses that are already closed because of stale cache entries.
3. **Service peaks and failures.** Load rises during the first week of each month, and the LLM sometimes does not respond. The design must support 10,000 or more incidents per week and access by 50-100 engineers while maintaining visibility and fault tolerance.

These are the three critical areas evaluated by [`../agents/eval-spec.md`](../agents/eval-spec.md).
