# 3. Objective

Reduce support workload and speed up a correct human response to ERP incidents through contextual assistance and traceable escalation. The flow must allow support to resolve user errors, engineering to receive the relevant technical cases, and any erroneous answer to be reopened without losing history.

Responses to *Customer Escalations* must meet the one-day SLA and responses to *Engineering Escalations* must meet the three-day SLA, as stated in the assignment. The time is measured from registration until the first substantive human response; total resolution time is tracked separately because the case does not set a closure deadline. A person always performs the solution.
