# 4. Out of scope

- Training or replacing the existing local LLM; the architecture's nightly update refers to the RAG knowledge index, not retraining model weights.
- Autonomous LLM actions on ERP systems, databases, code, or infrastructure; automatic ticket closure is also excluded.
- A client self-service portal, account registration, or a Harness-native login. Support and engineering use credentials supplied by the company.
- Fixing ERP products themselves or managing the client commercial lifecycle.
- Promising resolution of every ticket, exactly-once delivery of external messages, or a closure deadline not stated in the case.
