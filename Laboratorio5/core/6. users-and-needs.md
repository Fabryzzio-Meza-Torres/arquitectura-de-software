# 6. Users and needs

The three operational roles defined in [`../people/`](../people/) receive Balbuena-provided credentials; the Harness does not offer registration or a self-service login. The Harness attributes every action to the identity associated with those credentials.

| Role | Main needs |
| --- | --- |
| Eric, support | Register reports, consult suggestions and evidence, resolve simple cases, see live status, request information, escalate, and reopen with history. |
| Julia, engineering | Create internal incidents, receive escalations with context, investigate through knowledge and safe queries, record resolution or non-resolution, and review risky proposals. |
| Jesus, engineering manager | Receive escalations without duplicate assignments, distribute them, track deadlines and workload, and know which risky actions were blocked. |

A client who reports by email or phone is the ticket originator, not a direct Harness user in this laboratory.
