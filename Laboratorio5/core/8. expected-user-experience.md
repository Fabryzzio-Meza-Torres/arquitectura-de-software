# 8. Expected user experience

- Support registers a ticket with minimal information, receives its identifier, and sees its type, priority, deadline, and status. It can directly resolve a user error.
- For a difficult case, the Harness presents a proposal, sources, confidence, and actions requiring review. Support decides what to do and records its intervention; the LLM never declares the case resolved.
- If information is missing or the LLM fails, the interface presents a clear outcome: request data, continue human analysis, or escalate. The ticket never disappears from the queue.
- Engineering receives the history and escalation reasons, records its assessment and solution. Jesus sees pending tickets, owners, and deadlines, then distributes the work.
- When reopening a ticket, users see the previous solution, the reason it was incorrect, and the new owner. The displayed status updates after every change, without reusing a stale value.
