# Eric Biagioli - Support specialist

## Who he is

He receives incident reports by email or phone from Balbuena's domestic and international ERP clients. He handles many ERP user errors as well as cases that need investigation. He accesses the Harness with credentials supplied by the company; the product has no self-service registration or login.

## Needs from the system

1. Register client reports with minimal data and no duplicate tickets.
2. Distinguish a user error from a problem that requires LLM or engineering assistance, with visible priority and deadline.
3. Receive LLM proposals with sources and uncertainty to review; he performs the solution and decides whether to close the ticket.
4. See current status, request missing information, and have a fallback when the LLM does not respond.
5. Escalate to engineering with the complete history when he cannot resolve the issue, without duplicate notifications.
6. Reopen an incorrect solution with a reason and preserved history, and receive subsequent status changes.

## Risks and observable success

He is concerned about erroneous answers, stale statuses, demand peaks, and destructive actions. He can complete F1, F2, and F4 without losing the ticket or delegating the solution or closure to the LLM. The one-day SLA is for the first substantive human response, not total resolution.
