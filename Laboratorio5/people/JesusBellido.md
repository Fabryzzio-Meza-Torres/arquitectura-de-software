# Jesus Bellido - Engineering manager

## Who he is

He receives and distributes escalations to his team. He monitors workload, deadlines, and decisions for risky incidents. He accesses the Harness with credentials supplied by Balbuena, without registration or a self-service login.

## Needs from the system

1. Receive each escalation notification through Slack without an effective duplicate notification or assignment.
2. See why the ticket was escalated, what the LLM proposed, what failed, and which action was blocked.
3. Assign a responsible person and follow unassigned or unresolved tickets.
4. Monitor the one-day first human response for client-origin tickets and the three-day response for engineering-origin tickets.
5. Observe peaks and LLM failures to redistribute work and prioritize deep review.
6. Verify that the LLM performs no destructive solution and that human intervention is recorded.

## Risks and observable success

He is concerned about lost notifications, duplication, and the data deletion caused by the previous Harness. He can follow F2 and F5 and find either an owner or a visible pending item for every escalation.
