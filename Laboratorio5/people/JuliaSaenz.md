# Julia Saenz - Software engineer

## Who she is

She investigates technical engineering incidents and client tickets referred by support. She uses credentials supplied by Balbuena; the Harness has no self-service login.

## Needs from the system

1. Create engineering incidents and know their three-day response deadline.
2. Receive tickets escalated after Jesus assigns them, without duplicates and with their origin, history, and reasons.
3. Consult proposals, RAG sources, and authorized read-only data to investigate without repeating work.
4. Review risky proposals and personally perform any approved intervention, without autonomous LLM actions.
5. Record a resolution, closure, or lack of resolution with her identity and evidence.
6. Resume tickets reopened because of an erroneous response and contribute knowledge only after validating the solution.

## Risks and observable success

She is concerned about context-free escalations, stale data, and tests that affect production. She can complete F3 and F4 with safe read access, an isolated sandbox, and an auditable outcome.
