# Study case 5 - sources and clarifications

- [Lab5.pdf](Lab5.pdf) is the original assignment. It defines Genius-x, three incident types, the local LLM with an API, the database-deletion incident, erroneous or outdated answers, first-week peaks, 10,000 or more incidents per week, 50-100 engineers, one-day response SLAs for *Customer Escalations* and three-day response SLAs for *Engineering Escalations*, availability, fault tolerance, low latency for critical decisions, and an architecture diagram as a deliverable. The PDF's hints encourage identifying single points of failure and high-risk components; they are not new product functions.
- [contexto-problema.png](contexto-problema.png) and the request clarifications describe Balbuena Consulting Enterprise as an ERP provider for domestic and international clients. Support receives many user-error reports and is overloaded. Genius-x is the *harness* around an existing local LLM. The LLM helps investigate and propose; a person reviews and performs the solution and decides whether to close the ticket. The operational actors are in `../people/`. The company provides them with credentials, so the Harness has no self-service registration or login.
- [final_architecture.excalidraw](../diagram/final_architecture.excalidraw) is the architecture reference for this specification. Its drawing shows proposed components; any text that suggests autonomous LLM actions is interpreted under the human-review constraint above.

The PDF source and the user's clarifications are kept separate. The business context and the prohibition of autonomous solutions are instructions from the request, not claims attributed to the PDF.
